## What We Discussed

Ryan joins Larry Tuch on The Abundant Wallet to talk senior housing transitions, real estate valuation, aging in place vs assisted living, and why a fast solution is rarely the best one.

A few of the threads we pulled on:

- **Senior housing transitions, and where families quietly lose money.** The decisions that look like progress, a rushed listing or a quick-cash offer, are often the ones that cost the family the most equity.
- **What a parent's home is actually worth.** Real estate valuation past the Zillow estimate, down to the net proceeds number that should drive the decision.
- **Aging in place versus assisted living.** Running the real numbers on both paths instead of defaulting to whichever feels easier in the moment.
- **Why a fast solution is rarely the best one.** Speed feels like relief, but the families who plan ahead keep far more of what they have built.
