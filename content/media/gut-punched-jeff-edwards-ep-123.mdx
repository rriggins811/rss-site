## What We Discussed

This episode is about the decisions caregivers face when a diagnosis meets the family's money: the home, the savings, and how to pay for long-term care.

- **Whether to sell the house to pay for care.** Jeff shares his own experience weighing whether to sell his home to cover care, and how an elder care attorney showed him there were other options.
- **What the "we buy houses" side looks like from the inside.** I used to work on that side. Now I help families protect a parent's home, and we talked about cash offers from someone who has been on the buying end of them.
- **Whether to relocate a loved one for treatment.** A segment with Dr. John Huffaker looks at whether families should move a loved one with Alzheimer's to the United States for treatment.
- **Get qualified advice first.** The thread through the whole episode: before a major financial decision made in a crisis, talk to someone qualified to look at the whole picture.
