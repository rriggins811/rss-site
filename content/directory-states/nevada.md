# Nevada Senior Help Directory: Statewide Programs for Seniors and Families
*Free, plain-English help for seniors and families in Nevada. Education, not advice. Verify before you rely on it.*

## Start here (works anywhere in Nevada)
- 211 (dial 2-1-1), Eldercare Locator 1-800-677-1116, BenefitsCheckUp.org, Medicare 1-800-633-4227, Social Security 1-800-772-1213, VA 1-800-827-1000, Alzheimer's 24/7 Helpline 1-800-272-3900, National Elder Fraud Hotline 1-833-372-8311, Crisis Lifeline 988

## Free Medicare counseling (Nevada MAP / SHIP)
Nevada's SHIP program operates under the name **Medicare Assistance Program (MAP)**, administered by the Nevada Aging and Disability Services Division (ADSD).

- Statewide toll-free: 1-800-307-4444
- Southern Nevada (Las Vegas area): 702-616-4926
- Website: adsd.nv.gov/programs/seniors/ship/ship_prog/
- MAP counselors provide free, unbiased help with Medicare plan comparisons, Medicare Advantage, Part D drug coverage, Medigap, and billing issues. They do not sell insurance.

## Medicaid in Nevada
Nevada Medicaid is administered by the Division of Health Care Financing and Policy. Applications are processed through Access Nevada.

- Apply online: access.nv.gov or benefitsportal.acses.nv.gov
- Statewide: 1-800-992-0900
- Northern Nevada: 775-684-7200
- Southern Nevada: 702-486-1646
- For seniors needing long-term care, personal care services, or nursing home coverage: contact your local Area Agency on Aging or call Access Nevada to be connected with a Medicaid Long-Term Services and Supports (LTSS) coordinator; assessment and enrollment must occur through the Division of Aging

## Prescription help
Nevada does not operate a standalone state pharmaceutical assistance program (SPAP). Seniors on Medicare Part D should apply for **Extra Help** (the federal Low Income Subsidy).

- Apply for Extra Help: Social Security 1-800-772-1213 or ssa.gov
- NeedyMeds.org: manufacturer patient-assistance programs by drug name
- MAP counselors can assist with Part D comparisons: 1-800-307-4444

**Note:** Nevada's STAR (State Assistance for Rx) program officially concluded. The application deadline was October 31, 2016, and the program has not been accepting applications since that time (source: adsd.nv.gov/programs/seniors/ship/Star/).

## Energy and utility help
Nevada's LIHEAP program is called the **Energy Assistance Program (EAP)**, administered by the Nevada Division of Social Services (DSS).

- Website: dss.nv.gov/programs/energy
- Carson City (state administration): 775-684-0800
- Program year: July 1 through June 30 (funding-dependent; apply early)
- Local EAP offices are administered through community action agencies statewide; call 211 or visit dss.nv.gov for local office locations
- Benefits cover heating and cooling costs for qualifying low-income households

Utility companies also offer separate low-income programs: NV Energy's Energy Assistance Fund (EAF) and budget billing options. Contact your utility directly.

## Property tax relief (Nevada)
Nevada does not have a statewide senior-specific property tax exemption program currently available for application. The primary residential protections are:

**Residential Cap on Assessed Value Growth**
Under Nevada Revised Statutes 361.4723, property tax assessments on primary residences are capped at a 3% annual increase in assessed value (regardless of actual market appreciation). This applies automatically to owner-occupied primary residences with no application required. It is not an exemption but significantly limits property tax increases for long-term homeowners.

**Property Tax Exemption for Disabled Veterans and Surviving Spouses**
Veterans with a service-connected disability rating of 100% or surviving unremarried spouses may qualify for a partial property tax exemption. Contact your county assessor's office for current amounts and application deadlines.

**Disabled Persons Property Tax Exemption**
Persons who are legally blind or have a permanent and total disability may qualify. Contact your county assessor.

For all property tax exemptions, contact your county assessor directly (Clark County: 702-455-3882; Washoe County: 775-328-2277; all other counties: contact the county assessor listed at tax.nv.gov).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Nevada's SHIP called?**
Nevada calls its SHIP the Medicare Assistance Program (MAP). Call 1-800-307-4444 statewide or 702-616-4926 in Southern Nevada. It is free and counselors do not sell insurance.

**Does Nevada have a senior property tax exemption?**
Nevada does not currently have a general senior property tax discount program. The main protection for primary homeowners is the 3% annual cap on assessed value growth under NRS 361.4723, which applies automatically. Disabled veterans and persons with total disability may qualify for partial exemptions through their county assessor.

**How do Nevada seniors get help with utility bills?**
Through the Energy Assistance Program (EAP), Nevada's LIHEAP program. Call 211 to find your local EAP office or visit dss.nv.gov/programs/energy. The program year runs July 1 through June 30.

**How do I apply for Nevada Medicaid for long-term care?**
Call 1-800-992-0900 or visit access.nv.gov. For home and community-based services, contact your local Area Agency on Aging to discuss the Medicaid waiver process. Eligibility is based on income, assets, and a functional needs assessment.
