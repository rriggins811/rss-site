# Oregon Senior Help Directory: Statewide Programs for Seniors & Families
*Free, plain-English help for seniors and families in Oregon. Education, not advice. Verify before you rely on it.*

## Free Medicare counseling (Oregon SHIP)
Oregon's State Health Insurance Assistance Program is branded **SHIBA (Senior Health Insurance Benefits Assistance)**, a statewide network of trained, certified volunteer counselors run through Oregon state government. SHIBA gives free, unbiased one-on-one help with Medicare, Medicare Advantage, Part D drug plans, Medigap, enrollment, appeals, and fraud questions, and counselors do not sell insurance. Statewide line: **1-800-722-4134**. Online: **shiba.oregon.gov**. Email: shiba.oregon@odhs.oregon.gov.

## Medicaid in Oregon
Oregon's Medicaid program is the **Oregon Health Plan (OHP)**, run by the Oregon Health Authority. Apply online through the **ONE** system at **one.oregon.gov**, or by phone through **ONE Customer Service at 1-800-699-9075** (Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. Pacific). **Older adults and people with disabilities** can also apply through the **Aging and Disability Resource Connection (ADRC) at 1-855-673-2372 (1-855-ORE-ADRC)** or a local Oregon Department of Human Services office. OHP can help pay Medicare premiums and long-term care costs for those who qualify.

## Prescription help
Oregon runs the **Oregon Prescription Drug Program (OPDP)**, a free **discount card** from the Oregon Health Authority. It is open to **any Oregon resident with no age, income, or membership requirement** and offers savings on generic and brand medications at participating pharmacies (it is a discount, not insurance, and does not pay a fixed share of the cost). Enroll for free at **oregon.gov/oha/hpa/dsi-opdp** or by phone at **1-800-913-4146**. For deeper help, call **SHIBA at 1-800-722-4134**, which screens you for **Medicare Extra Help (the Low-Income Subsidy)**, and use **BenefitsCheckUp.org** for manufacturer patient-assistance programs.

## Energy and utility help
Oregon runs LIHEAP through **Oregon Housing and Community Services (OHCS)**, delivered by local Community Action Agencies. LIHEAP helps pay home energy costs for households at or below **60 percent of Oregon's median income**, with grants paid directly to your utility; seniors 60 and older and people with disabilities get priority, and crisis cases (shutoff notice or broken heat) are expedited. To apply, contact your **local Community Action Agency** (call **1-800-453-5511, option 2**, or find your agency at oregon.gov/ohcs), or dial **211**. Both homeowners and renters may apply. There is no fixed annual deadline, but funds are first-come, first-served.

## Property tax relief (Oregon)
Oregon does not have a general senior homestead exemption. Its main senior program is a **deferral**, so read the caution below before you sign.
- **Oregon Property Tax Deferral for Disabled and Senior Homeowners (ORS 311.668), through the Oregon Department of Revenue and your county assessor**: the state **pays your property taxes to the county each November 15 and records a lien on your home**; the deferred taxes plus **6 percent annual interest** become due when the home is sold, the owner moves out, or the owner passes away. To qualify for the **2026** program you must be **at least 62 years old by April 15** (or receiving federal Social Security disability), have **household income of $70,000 or less** (2025 income), have **owned and lived in the home for at least 5 consecutive years**, and have a **net worth under $500,000** (not counting the home or personal property). Apply with the **Oregon Department of Revenue** (forms at oregon.gov/dor), generally by **April 15**; late filings run April 16 to December 1 with a county late fee.
- **One caution (important):** this is a deferral, not forgiveness. It can be a lifeline for someone who is house-rich and cash-poor, but the lien and the 6 percent interest reduce what the family keeps when the home is sold or inherited. Understand the trade-off, and talk it over with family, before applying.

## Food, in-home help, caregiver, transportation, and legal help
Oregon's Area Agencies on Aging and the statewide **Aging and Disability Resource Connection (ADRC) at 1-855-673-2372** arrange home-delivered meals, in-home care, caregiver support, and senior transportation. You can also reach your local agency through the **Eldercare Locator (1-800-677-1116)** or by dialing **211**. For free civil legal help, contact **Legal Aid Services of Oregon** or the **Oregon Senior Law Project** through your ADRC or 211.

## Frequently asked questions
**What is Oregon's SHIP program?** It is SHIBA (Senior Health Insurance Benefits Assistance), Oregon's free Medicare counseling network. Reach it at 1-800-722-4134.

**Does Oregon offer senior property tax relief?** Oregon has no general senior exemption, but it offers the Property Tax Deferral for Disabled and Senior Homeowners: the state pays your taxes and records a lien repaid with 6 percent interest when the home is sold. For 2026 you must be 62 by April 15, have household income of $70,000 or less, 5 years of ownership and residency, and net worth under $500,000.

**Does Oregon help seniors pay for prescriptions?** Oregon offers the free Oregon Prescription Drug Program discount card (no age or income limit) at 1-800-913-4146, and SHIBA can screen you for Medicare Extra Help.

**How do Oregon seniors get help with energy bills?** Through LIHEAP, run by Oregon Housing and Community Services and delivered by local Community Action Agencies. Call 1-800-453-5511 option 2, or dial 211.
