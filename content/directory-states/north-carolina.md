# North Carolina Senior Help Directory: Statewide Programs for Seniors & Families

*Free, plain-English help for seniors and families across North Carolina. Education, not advice. Verify program details before relying on them.*

## Start here (works anywhere in NC)
- **NC 211** - dial 2-1-1. Free, 24/7. Routes you to local rent, utility, food, and medical help.
- **Eldercare Locator** - 1-800-677-1116. Federal front door to your Area Agency on Aging, Adult Protective Services, and the long-term care ombudsman.
- **BenefitsCheckUp** - benefitscheckup.org. Screens you for every benefit you may qualify for.
- **Medicare** - 1-800-633-4227 | **Social Security** - 1-800-772-1213 | **VA Benefits** - 1-800-827-1000
- **Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline** - 1-800-272-3900 | **National Elder Fraud Hotline** - 1-833-372-8311 | **Crisis Lifeline** - 988

## Free Medicare counseling (NC SHIIP)
North Carolina's program is **SHIIP, the Seniors' Health Insurance Information Program**, run by the NC Department of Insurance. Free, unbiased one-on-one Medicare help: **1-855-408-1212.**

## Prescriptions
**NC MedAssist** runs a free pharmacy program for uninsured North Carolina residents under the income limit: **1-866-331-1348.**

## Energy / utility help
**LIEAP** (annual heating payment) and the **Crisis Intervention Program (CIP)** for heating/cooling emergencies are run through your **county Department of Social Services.** Seniors 60+ and disabled adults get a priority sign-up window. Find your county office through NC 211 or the NEAR line at 1-866-674-6327.

## Property tax relief (North Carolina)
NC offers three programs, applied for at your **county tax office** (deadline June 1):
- **Homestead Exclusion** - greater of $25,000 or 50% off taxable value; 2026 income limit $38,800; age 65+ or totally and permanently disabled.
- **Circuit Breaker** - caps tax at 4-5% of income; income limit $58,200. CAUTION: it is a deferral that becomes a lien at 6% interest, due when the home is sold or the owner passes.
- **Disabled Veteran Exclusion** - $45,000 off taxable value; no income limit, no age requirement.

## Home repair, food, transportation, caregiver, and legal help
Reach NC's Meals on Wheels, in-home aide services, the Family Caregiver Support Program, and senior transportation through the **Eldercare Locator (1-800-677-1116)** or **NC 211.** For free civil legal help, contact **Legal Aid of North Carolina.** For home repair, ask about **USDA Section 504** grants (homeowners 62+) and county weatherization programs.
