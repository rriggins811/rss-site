# New Jersey Senior Help Directory: Statewide Programs for Seniors & Families
*Free, plain-English help for seniors and families in New Jersey. Education, not advice. Verify before you rely on it.*

## Start here (works anywhere in New Jersey)
- **211** (dial 2-1-1) for local rent, utility, food, and medical help, 24/7
- **Eldercare Locator** 1-800-677-1116, the federal front door to your Area Agency on Aging
- **BenefitsCheckUp.org** screens you for every benefit you may qualify for
- **Medicare** 1-800-633-4227 | **Social Security** 1-800-772-1213 | **VA Benefits** 1-800-827-1000
- **Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline** 1-800-272-3900 | **National Elder Fraud Hotline** 1-833-372-8311 | **Crisis Lifeline** 988

## Free Medicare counseling (New Jersey SHIP)
New Jersey's program is the **State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP)**, run by the NJ Division of Aging Services. Free, unbiased one-on-one Medicare counseling, plan comparisons, and help with claims and appeals. Statewide toll-free hotline: **1-800-792-8820** (weekdays 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.). Counseling sites operate in all 21 counties. Online: nj.gov/humanservices/doas/services/q-z/ship.

## Medicaid in New Jersey
New Jersey Medicaid is called **NJ FamilyCare**. Apply online at **njfamilycare.org** (the fastest way) or call **1-800-701-0710** (TTY 711) to apply by phone with a Health Benefits Coordinator. NJ FamilyCare can help pay Medicare premiums and long-term care costs for those who qualify. Applications are processed within 45 days.

## Prescription help
New Jersey runs one of the strongest state drug programs in the country:
- **PAAD (Pharmaceutical Assistance to the Aged & Disabled)**: copays of $5 per covered generic and $7 per covered brand-name prescription for qualifying seniors 65+ (and disabled residents).
- **Senior Gold Prescription Discount Program**: for seniors whose income is above the PAAD limit but within $10,000 of it, copays are $15 plus half the remaining cost.
- Apply for both (plus other savings like Medicare Savings Programs) through the single **NJSave application** at nj.gov/humanservices/doas/services/l-p/njsave. Income limits adjust every year, so apply even if you were over the limit before.

## Energy and utility help
New Jersey's LIHEAP program is the **Home Energy Assistance Program (HEA)**, run by the NJ Department of Community Affairs (one application also covers the Universal Service Fund). Apply online at **dcaid.dca.nj.gov** or call the **HEA Hotline at 1-800-510-3102** (Mon-Fri 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.) to find your local application agency.

## Property tax relief (New Jersey)
New Jersey seniors now apply for THREE programs with ONE combined application, form **PAS-1**, filed online at **propertytaxrelief.nj.gov**. For the 2025 benefit year the deadline is **November 2, 2026**.
- **Senior Freeze (Property Tax Reimbursement)**: reimburses eligible homeowners 65+ (or receiving Social Security disability) for property tax increases above their "base year." Income limits: **$168,268 or less for 2024 and $172,475 or less for 2025**. 2025 payments began going out July 15, 2026.
- **Stay NJ**: new benefit for homeowners **65 and older** with income **under $500,000** who owned and lived in their home all 12 months of 2025. Pays **50% of your property tax bill up to a $6,500 cap** for 2025, calculated after ANCHOR and Senior Freeze, paid by check in quarterly installments.
- **ANCHOR**: the broader homeowner/renter rebate, included automatically in the same PAS-1 application.
File once, and the Division of Taxation calculates all three. Questions: the NJ Division of Taxation property tax relief pages at nj.gov/treasury/taxation/relief.shtml.

## Food, in-home help, caregiver, transportation, and legal help
The **NJ Division of Aging Services (nj.gov/humanservices/doas)** oversees the county-based Area Agencies on Aging (also called Aging & Disability Resource Connections), which handle home-delivered meals, in-home care, caregiver support, and senior transportation in all 21 counties. Find yours through the **Eldercare Locator (1-800-677-1116)** or by dialing **211**. For free civil legal help, ask your county Area Agency on Aging or 211 for the legal services office covering your county.

## Frequently asked questions
**What is New Jersey's SHIP program?** The State Health Insurance Assistance Program, run by the NJ Division of Aging Services. Free Medicare counseling at 1-800-792-8820, with sites in all 21 counties.

**Does New Jersey offer senior property tax relief?** Yes, three programs: Senior Freeze, Stay NJ (50% of property taxes up to $6,500 for 65+ homeowners with income under $500,000), and ANCHOR. One combined PAS-1 application at propertytaxrelief.nj.gov covers all three. The 2025 deadline is November 2, 2026.

**How do New Jersey seniors get help with prescription costs?** Through PAAD ($5 generic / $7 brand copays) or Senior Gold. Apply with the NJSave application through the NJ Division of Aging Services.

**How do New Jersey seniors get help with heating bills?** Through the Home Energy Assistance Program. Apply at dcaid.dca.nj.gov or call 1-800-510-3102.
