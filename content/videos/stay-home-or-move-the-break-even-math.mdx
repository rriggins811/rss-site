Most families choose stay home or move on a feeling and never run the math. That's how the money gets away from you. A late July report found nearly two out of three families now see home care as a long-term answer, not a stopgap.

Good instinct. But here's the part you don't want to skip. The cost isn't the grab bars and the ramp.

Those are one time. The real question is how many hours of help your mom needs in year three, not year one. After about 10 hours a week, staying home usually wins.

Past 30 hours a week, assisted living is often cheaper. All in. So run your real numbers before you make the emotional call.
