Your dad has a stroke, you walk into his bank to handle things, and they tell you no. That's when most families find out there's no power of attorney on file. No POA means no authority, so you're headed to court for guardianship where the bills stack up.

Four documents close that gap. A durable financial power of attorney, a healthcare power of attorney, an advance directive, and an up-to-date will. Most families have an old will from the 90s and none of the other three.

Here's your homework this weekend. Ask your parents one question. If you couldn't handle your own money for 90 days, who signs?
