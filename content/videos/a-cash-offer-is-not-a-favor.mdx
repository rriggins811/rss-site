When I was on the buying side, a paid-off house owned by an older person was easy money. I knew it before I knew the family's name. No mortgage means fast equity, and a tired or grieving owner takes a clean and fast number over the right number.

Speed was the whole game, but faster I moved, the less time anyone had to check what the place was worth. States are finally catching up. Ohio and Pennsylvania both passed laws to slow these deals down.

But you don't need a law to protect your folks. Never sign on the first visit. Get the real net number in writing.

Have one trusted person read the contract first, preferably an attorney or at least a realtor. A cash offer is not a favor. It is a business model, and you are the margin.
