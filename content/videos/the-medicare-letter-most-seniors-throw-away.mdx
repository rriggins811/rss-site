This one is for you if you are the one on Medicare. In September, your plan mails you a booklet called the Annual Notice of Change. Most folks glance at the cover and toss it.

Do not. That letter is how your plan changes your coverage for next year without a single phone call, new costs, dropped drugs, a doctor who is suddenly out of network. It is all in there, and it takes effect January 1st.

Whether you read it or not, you have read every important letter that ever landed in your mailbox. Read this one too, and compare your options on the planfinder at medicare. gov or call 1-800-MEDICARE.

Open enrollment runs October 15th to December 7th. You took care of your family for years. This is one more way to stay in the driver's seat.
