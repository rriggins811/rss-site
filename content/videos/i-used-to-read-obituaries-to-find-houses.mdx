I used to read obituaries for work. Not to pay respects. To find houses.

Here is what nobody tells you. When your parent dies without a will, that estate file is public the day it opens. The name, the address, the house. In North Carolina, if nobody entitled to administer applies within thirty days, the clerk of superior court can send a notice to qualify. At ninety days the clerk can declare your family's rights renounced and hand the estate to some suitable person. Suitable does not have to mean you.

Washington's attorney general took seven people to court over exactly that. 213 strangers' estates.
