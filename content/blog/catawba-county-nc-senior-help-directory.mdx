*Free, plain-English help for seniors and families in Catawba County, NC. Education, not advice. Verify before you rely on it.*

## Property tax relief (apply with the Catawba County Tax Department, 828-465-8436)

**The office.** Senior and disability relief is handled by the **Catawba County Tax Department**, **25 Government Drive, Newton, NC 28658**. Applications go by mail to **Assessor's Office, Attn: Tax Relief, Box 368, Newton, NC 28658-0368**, or in person at the Tax Department. **For an appointment or more information, call 828-465-8436**; the county's 2026 relief flyer says to call that number to **request an application**. The main Tax Department line is **828-465-8414**, and the state's official list of county assessors (revised September 23, 2026) gives **828-465-8408** and fax **828-465-8413** for the county Assessor. The county does not post counter hours, so call before you drive.

**The three programs.** All three are set by the state and filed on the state's **Form AV-9** with the county. The county's flyer says income means **2025 household income, including gross Social Security**.

- **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion.** Takes **up to half of your home's assessed value plus up to one acre of homesite, or $25,000, whichever is greater**. You must be **65 or older or totally and permanently disabled**, and household income must be within the limit: **$38,800 for 2026, $39,900 for 2027**. **You do not need to reapply every year**, but you **must notify the Tax Assessor if you move, sell or rent the house**. For disability, a physician completes **Form AV-9A** (the county page mislabels it "AV-9V" but links the right form).
- **Circuit Breaker Tax Deferment.** Limits the tax to **4 percent of income at $38,800 or less** and **5 percent between $38,800 and $58,200** for 2026 (the 2027 ceiling is **$59,850**). You must have **owned and occupied the home for 5 or more years**. **This is a deferral, not forgiveness.** The county says that on **death of the owner, transfer of the property, or the owner no longer living there**, the deferred taxes **come due for the current year plus the previous 3 years, with interest**. And because it is based on yearly income, **you must file a new application each year.** Understand that before your parent signs, especially if the plan is to sell the house or leave it to family.
- **Disabled Veteran Exclusion.** Takes **up to $45,000** off the assessed value for an honorably discharged veteran with a **100 percent total and permanent service-connected disability**, or an unmarried surviving spouse. **There is no income limit**, and you **do not reapply every year**. The **NCDVA-9** form is completed with a Veterans Service Officer (below).

**Deadline.** The county says to file **no later than June 1**.

**The 2027 revaluation is coming.** Catawba County has been on a **four-year revaluation cycle since 1999**. Today's values took effect **January 1, 2023** and were mailed in early February that year. The county says the **next revaluation is effective January 1, 2027**, and that the **next opportunity to appeal is January 2027**. Office appeals that are not settled can go to the **Board of Equalization and Review**, then the **NC Property Tax Commission**. The county has **not posted the 2027 notice date** yet. Questions: **828-465-8436**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., or Revaluation@catawbacountync.gov.

The county's pages do not say how a revaluation affects relief that is already approved, so call **828-465-8436** once the new value arrives to confirm your parent's status. If a parent's income is under the 2027 limit and they are not yet on the exclusion, **June 1, 2027** is the date to circle.

**Rates.** The county rate on **2026 bills is 39.85 cents per $100** of value, the same as every year since 2023. Towns add their own: **Hickory 46 cents**, **Newton 45**, **Conover 39.5**, **Maiden 38**, **Claremont 41.75**, **Long View 49**, **Brookford 40**, and the **Town of Catawba 50**. For this year's due date, payment arrangements or a bill question, call the Tax Department at **828-465-8414**; the county's current pages do not post them.

**Two protections worth ten minutes.**
- The **Register of Deeds** links to a free **Property Notification** service (ncpropertynotification.org, run by the county's records vendor) that tells you **when a name you list shows up in recorded documents**. The office is at **100 Government Drive, Newton**, **828-465-1573**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
- The Register of Deeds also records a veteran's **DD-214 free of charge**. It must be done **in person**. A recorded DD-214 is much easier to find years later when a family is sorting out benefits.

## Food

Meals come to the home **four days a week**, with **frozen meals** for the other days and monthly **pet food** for meal clients' pets. It is for anyone in Catawba County who is **60 or older, homebound**, and has **no one in the household able or willing to shop or cook**. **There are no income requirements and no cost** (donations are accepted). Call **828-695-5610**, Monday to Friday 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. The page does not mention a waitlist, so ask when you call.

If you have an old flyer for **Eastern Catawba Cooperative Christian Ministry**, this is the same group under the name it took in **February 2024**.

## Energy and utility help

**Where to apply: Ashure Ministry.** Ashure takes **Low Income Energy Assistance (LIEAP)**, **Crisis Intervention Program (CIP)** and **General Assistance** applications for Catawba County residents **on behalf of Catawba County Social Services**, **Monday to Thursday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.**, at **245 East N Street, Newton, 828-465-1702**.

**LIEAP.** A once-a-year payment toward heating. The state says households with **someone 60 or older**, or a disabled person getting services through the NC Division of Aging and Adult Services, can sign up **December 1 to 31**; everyone else applies **January 1 to March 31** or until the money runs out. It is based on **130 percent** of the poverty level.

**CIP.** For heating or cooling emergencies such as a **disconnect notice**, taken **July 1 to June 30**. The state sets income at **150 percent** of poverty, and households with a member **60 or older** or disabled can have **resources up to $4,500**. Catawba County does not post a dollar cap, so ask Ashure what this year's maximum is.

**Greater Hickory Cooperative Christian Ministry (GHCCM).** **31 1st Avenue SE, Hickory, 828-327-0979**, Monday to Thursday 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. and Friday 8 a.m. to noon. It helps with **utility disconnection prevention**, **eviction prevention (rent and mortgage)** and other crisis needs **case by case, depending on funds**. Bring a photo ID, proof of address, **4 weeks of household income**, expenses, a bank statement, and the **disconnect or eviction notice**.

**If the bill says City of Newton.** Newton's **CARE** program, run with **Ashure Ministry**, helps Newton utility customers with bills **in times of extreme need**. Newton Customer Service is **828-695-4300**; call **before** the due date to ask about a payment plan. Newton also runs a **Gatekeeper Program** to look out for elderly citizens: **828-695-4279**.

**If the bill says Town of Maiden.** Maiden runs its own electric system. Call **828-428-5000** (8 a.m. to 5 p.m.) or **828-464-3112** after hours, and ask Ashure about help with the bill.

**Too hot, not just too cold.** The state's **Operation Fan Heat Relief** gives fans, and air conditioners for some qualifying medical conditions, to people **60 and older** and adults with disabilities, but only **through October 31, 2026**. Catawba County's providers are the **Council on Aging (828-328-2269)** and **Ashure Ministry (828-465-1702)**.

## Medicare, Medicaid, prescriptions

**Free Medicare counseling.** North Carolina's **Seniors' Health Insurance Information Program (SHIIP)**, run by the NC Department of Insurance, gives free, unbiased Medicare counseling. The state's county locator lists the **Catawba County Council on Aging, 400 17th Street SW, Hickory, 828-328-2269**, **by appointment only** (ask for SHIIP help). The Council offers counselors in **Hickory, Newton and Sherrills Ford**. The statewide SHIIP line is **1-855-408-1212**. Call before any Medicare Advantage or Part D decision, especially during open enrollment, **October 15 to December 7, 2026**.

**Help paying Medicare costs and prescriptions.** The **Medicare Savings Programs** can pay Medicare premiums, and **Extra Help** lowers Part D drug costs. Apply for the Medicare Savings Programs through Social Services' adult Medicaid line, **828-695-5692**, and for Extra Help through SHIIP or Social Security at **1-800-772-1213**. **NC MedAssist (1-866-331-1348)** is a free prescription program for uninsured North Carolinians under 300 percent of poverty and says it may help **some Medicare patients case by case**. **GHCCM's pharmacy (828-323-7913)** fills some 30-day prescriptions for **$4**, but only for current **Kintegra Health** patients or people with a **GHCCM Healthcare Card**. We are not printing income limits for the Medicare programs; ask SHIIP to check them against your parent's numbers.

**Medicaid, and a reason to apply before December 31.** Apply by phone at **828-695-5692**, online, in person or by mail. The **NC Medicaid Contact Center** is **888-245-0179**. NC Medicaid says that **if you apply by December 31, 2026, Medicaid will cover approved costs for up to 3 months before you applied**. Starting in 2027, that window shrinks to **2 months for seniors and people with disabilities**. If a parent is heading toward a nursing home or already paying for care, that difference can be real money.

**Staying home on Medicaid: CAP/DA.** The **Community Alternatives Program for Disabled Adults (CAP/DA)** is North Carolina's Medicaid waiver that brings in-home aide help, respite and case management so someone who would otherwise qualify for a nursing home can stay home. NC Medicaid's list of case management agencies for Catawba County (dated May 2025) names **Catawba County DSS, 828-695-5609**; **KBHR Consulting Services, 704-962-1267**; **Lincoln County DSS, 704-732-0738**; **Primary Health Choice, 910-865-3500**; **RHA Management Services, 888-207-7828**; and **Walkers Home Care CAPDA, 252-432-6308**. The list is more than a year old, so if a line does not answer, start with DSS.

**Special Assistance In-Home.** Help for **Medicaid-eligible** adults with income **at or below 100 percent of poverty** whose doctor verifies they need adult care home or group home level care, so they can stay home instead. Ask Adult Services at **828-695-5609**.

**Mental health.** Catawba County's public mental health plan is **Partners Health Management**. Its **Behavioral Health Crisis Line is 1-833-353-2093, 24 hours a day, every day**; the member and recipient services line is **1-888-235-4673**, Monday to Saturday 7 a.m. to 6 p.m.

## Home repair and safety

- **Urgent Repair Program 2026.** Catawba County has **$150,000** in Urgent Repair funds to help **at least 12 homeowners** with **roofs, heating and air, no heat source, and porches**. It is for owners who **live in the home**, are **very low income**, and have a special need, which includes being **62 or older**, a household member with a disability, or a veteran. The 2026 income limits are **$20,550 for one person** and **$23,500 for two**. The Western Piedmont Council of Governments runs it; the county's notice names **Laurie Powell, 828-485-4249**. The notice gives no deadline, so call soon.
- **Habitat for Humanity of Catawba Valley.** Its repair program covers roofs, heating and air, ramps, porches and steps, and safety plumbing and electrical for owners who have lived in the home **at least a year**, with income **at or below 80 percent** of area median. The owner pays **two payments, each 10 percent of gross monthly income**. **It is not taking applications right now** and opens about **once or twice a year**; fill out its inquiry form to be told about the next round, or call **828-328-4663**. It cannot respond to emergencies.
- **Weatherization and heating repair.** North Carolina's weatherization provider for Catawba County is **Blue Ridge Community Action, 800 North Green Street, Morganton, 828-438-6255**. It is **not** run out of Hickory. Weatherization serves households **below 200 percent** of poverty, which the state lists as **$31,920 a year for one person and $43,280 for two**; households on **SSI or Work First** qualify automatically. Its **Heating and Air Repair and Replacement Program (HARRP)** fixes or replaces failing systems, but SSI does **not** qualify a household automatically for HARRP. Blue Ridge says its **programs may have waitlists**.

**The emergency registry.** Catawba County keeps a **Special Needs Registry** for people who would need help in a disaster; sign up with the online form on the county's Emergency Services forms page. Emergency Management's office line is **828-465-8230**. The county also runs a **Community Alert System** (CodeRED) for emergency calls and texts, linked from its Emergency Services resources page.

## Transportation

**Greenway buses: 60 cents at 65.** **Greenway Public Transportation** (Western Piedmont Regional Transit Authority) runs **seven fixed routes** in **Hickory, Newton and Conover**, **Monday to Friday 5:45 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.** and on **Saturdays** (check the posted schedule), with **no reservation needed**. Routes reach Catawba Valley Medical Center, Social Services and Public Health, the Justice Center, the Hickory VA and the West Hickory Senior Center. The regular fare is **$1.25**; riders **65 and older, people with disabilities and Medicare card holders pay 60 cents** with a Medicare card or a Greenway half-fare ID, which you get at the Greenway office, **1459 Robinwood Road, Newton**. Tickets are **cash only**. Route information: **828-465-7634**.

**Greenway ADA paratransit.** Curb-to-curb rides within **three quarters of a mile** of a bus route for riders certified through an ADA application, **$2.50 each way**. Weekdays 5:45 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and Saturdays 8:45 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., **no Sunday service**. Book **the day before**, between 8:30 a.m. and 5 p.m. Monday to Friday, at **828-464-9444**. **No same-day rides.**

**Rides outside the bus routes.** Greenway also runs **shared, curb-to-curb rides for the general public** anywhere in Alexander, Burke, Caldwell and Catawba counties, **Monday to Friday 5:30 a.m. to 6 p.m.**, **$2.50 each way** (**$5** out of the service area). Book by **noon the business day before** at **828-464-9444**.

**Medicaid rides to appointments.** Social Services' Medicaid Transportation unit drives Medicaid members to covered appointments: **828-695-5608**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Give **3 business days notice** for appointments in Catawba County and **5 business days** for trips outside the county.

## Legal help

**Senior Legal Helpline.** **Legal Aid of North Carolina's Senior Legal Helpline** gives **free civil legal help to North Carolinians 60 and older**: **1-877-579-7562**, Monday to Friday **9 to 11 a.m. and 1 to 3 p.m.** Legal Aid's Senior Law Project gives priority to those with the greatest economic need and helps with wills and powers of attorney, Medicaid and other benefits, abuse and neglect, and housing.

**Legal Aid of North Carolina, Foothills office.** **211 East Union Street, Morganton**, which serves Catawba County: **828-437-8280** or toll free **1-800-849-5195**.

**Protecting an older adult.**
- **Catawba County Adult Protective Services:** **828-695-5609**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. **After hours and on holidays, call 911.** North Carolina law requires anyone who suspects a disabled adult is being abused, neglected or exploited to report it.
- **Long-Term Care Ombudsman at Western Piedmont**, for problems in a nursing home or assisted living. Call the Area Agency on Aging at **828-322-9191** and ask for the regional ombudsman who covers Catawba County.
- **NC Attorney General Consumer Protection: 1-877-566-7226** (1-877-5-NO-SCAM).
- **National Elder Fraud Hotline: 1-833-372-8311.**

## PACE

**Catawba County has a PACE program, and its center is in Newton.**

PACE, the **Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly**, takes over a person's whole care plan, doctors, a day center, therapy, personal care, prescriptions and transportation, so someone who needs a nursing home level of care can keep living at home. **Senior TLC**, which was **PACE @Home** until its recent name change, runs its center at **1915 Fairgrove Church Road SE, Newton, NC 28658**: main line **980-308-0858**, toll free **855-327-7852**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The state's PACE list gives **828-468-3980**.

**Who qualifies.** Medicare says PACE is for people **55 and older** who **live in a PACE service area**, **need a nursing home level of care as certified by the state**, and **can live safely in the community** with PACE's help.

**Check the ZIP code.** Senior TLC's own service area page says it covers **all Catawba County ZIP codes**. NC Medicaid's official PACE list (October 2025) names the program for **Catawba and Alexander counties and portions of Lincoln, Burke and Caldwell**, but its ZIP code list for Catawba includes only **Hickory 28601 and 28602**, **28612**, **Hildebran 28637**, **Maiden 28650** and **Sherrills Ford 28673**. It does **not** list **Newton 28658**, **Conover 28613**, **Claremont 28610**, **Catawba 28609**, **Terrell 28682** or Hickory **28603**. Confirm your parent's address with Senior TLC when you call.

## Frequently asked questions
**Mom is 72, lives in Conover, and gets about $2,900 a month from Social Security and a small pension. Does she qualify for a tax break?** Likely. About $2,900 a month is about $34,800 a year, under the **$38,800** limit for 2026 and the **$39,900** limit for 2027, as long as that is the whole household's income, counting gross Social Security. The **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion** takes the greater of **$25,000 or half** her home's assessed value off. The application is due **by June 1**. Call **828-465-8436** to request an application.

**Dad's house gets a new value in 2027. What should we watch for?** The county says the revaluation is effective **January 1, 2027**, and that is when the next appeal window opens. Last time, notices went out in early February. If the value looks wrong, call **828-465-8436** right away. If he uses the **Circuit Breaker**, he files again every year anyway.

**Should my father use the Circuit Breaker instead?** Go in with open eyes. It limits his tax to 4 or 5 percent of income, but the rest is **deferred, not forgiven**: at his death, a sale or transfer, or a move, **the current year plus the previous 3 years come due with interest**. He needs **5 years** of ownership and occupancy and must **reapply every year**. If his income is under the exclusion limit, the exclusion is usually simpler. Ask the Tax Department to run both, and talk to the **Senior Legal Helpline (1-877-579-7562)** if the house is part of an estate plan.

**Mom's savings are running out and she may need Medicaid for care. Does timing matter?** It can. NC Medicaid says applications filed **by December 31, 2026** can get approved costs covered for **up to 3 months before** the application; in 2027 that drops to **2 months** for seniors. Call Social Services' adult Medicaid line at **828-695-5692**, and talk to the Senior Legal Helpline before moving any assets.

**Mom lives alone in Newton and I live out of state. What can I set up today?** Add her to the county's **Special Needs Registry** and the **CodeRED** alerts. Sign her up for the Register of Deeds **Property Notification** service. Call **Senior Nutrition Services (828-695-5610)** about Meals on Wheels or Seniors Morning Out, and ask the **Council on Aging (828-328-2269)** about Telephone Reassurance. Newton is on the **Greenway** bus routes, 60 cents at 65. If the bill says City of Newton, ask about the **Gatekeeper Program (828-695-4279)**. And if she needs a nursing home level of care, ask **Senior TLC (980-308-0858)** whether PACE can keep her home.
