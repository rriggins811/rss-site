    answer: "You claim them with the Mobile County Revenue Commissioner, currently Kim Hastie, at 3925 Michael Boulevard, Suite G, Mobile, AL 36609, phone 251-574-8530. *Free, plain-English help for seniors and families in Mobile County, AL. Education, not advice. Verify before you rely on it.*

## Start here (works anywhere)
- 211 (dial 2-1-1), Eldercare Locator 1-800-677-1116, BenefitsCheckUp.org, Medicare 1-800-633-4227, Social Security 1-800-772-1213, VA 1-800-827-1000, Alzheimer's 24/7 Helpline 1-800-272-3900, National Elder Fraud Hotline 1-833-372-8311, Crisis Lifeline 988

**Three local front doors:** **South Alabama Regional Planning Commission Area Agency on Aging (Aging South Alabama)**, **110 Beauregard Street, Mobile, AL 36602**, phone **251-706-4680**, or statewide **1-800-AGE-LINE (1-800-243-5463)** | **Mobile County Revenue Commissioner** for property tax exemptions, **3925 Michael Boulevard, Suite G, Mobile, AL 36609**, **251-574-8530** | **2-1-1**.

**What makes this county different.** Jefferson, Shelby and Madison correctly said PACE does not reach their half of Alabama. **Mobile is where PACE does reach.** Alabama Medicaid limits the service area to **Mobile and Baldwin counties**, and **Mercy LIFE of Alabama** runs it from Springhill Avenue. The AAA and **Wave Transit** customer service share the **GM&O building** at 110 Beauregard. **Mobile Community Action runs both LIHEAP and weatherization**, unlike Shelby's split agencies.

**Office title.** Madison uses a Tax Assessor. Shelby uses a Property Tax Commissioner. ADOR lists Mobile with a **Revenue Commissioner**, currently **Kim Hastie**. Call **251-574-8530**.

**Reclaim rule.** Mobile publishes that **H-2 and H-3 age/income exemptions, disability H-3, and blindness exemptions must be reclaimed by December 31**. H-3 owners get a renewal form in October **in lieu of a tax bill**. Miss it and the exemption cancels.

**Naming trap.** City of Mobile vs Mobile County (millage differs by municipality). There is also a Mobile County in Florida. If a number does not start with **251**, check the state.

Also on the fridge: **Alabama Adult Abuse Hotline 1-800-458-7214**, Long-Term Care Ombudsman via AAA **251-706-4680**, and **2-1-1**.

## Property tax relief (Revenue Commissioner, 251-574-8530)
**Kim Hastie, Revenue Commissioner**, **3925 Michael Boulevard / Michael Square, Suite G, Mobile, AL 36609**, **251-574-8530**, mail PO Box/Drawer 1169, Mobile, AL 36633-1169. Main hours published Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., **closed Wednesdays**. Call before driving to a satellite.

Taxes **due October 1**, delinquent after **December 31**. Status as of October 1.

Mobile exemption codes: **H1** standard homestead; **H4** over 65 income too high for deeper breaks (all **state** exempt); **H2** all state plus up to **$5,000** county (state AGI $12,000 or less); **H3** total state/county/city exempt (federal taxable income $12,000 or less at 65+, or permanent total disability with published proof). **H2/H3/disability/blind must be reclaimed yearly by December 31.**

Statewide **Act 2024-344** 7 percent assessed-value cap for Class II/III through FY beginning October 1, 2027.

**Routed:** satellite open status this week; local acts beyond H tiers; parcel millage. Call **251-574-8530**.
