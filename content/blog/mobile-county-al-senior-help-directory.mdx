faqs:
  - question: "Where do I start finding senior help in Mobile County, AL?"
    answer: "Three front doors, and two of them share the same downtown building. First, the South Alabama Regional Planning Commission Area Agency on Aging, also called Aging South Alabama, at 110 Beauregard Street, Mobile, AL 36602, phone 251-706-4680, or through the statewide line 1-800-AGE-LINE (1-800-243-5463). That call reaches SHIP Medicare counseling, Meals on Wheels intake, Alabama Cares caregiver support, legal counseling for people 60 and older, and options counseling for the Medicaid Elderly and Disabled Waiver. Second, dial 2-1-1 for whatever has money this week. Third, for property tax exemptions only, the Mobile County Revenue Commissioner at 3925 Michael Boulevard, Suite G, phone 251-574-8530."
  - question: "What property tax breaks can a senior get in Mobile County, AL?"
    answer: "You claim them with the Mobile County Revenue Commissioner, currently Kim Hastie, at 3925 Michael Boulevard, Suite G, Mobile, AL 36609, phone 251-574-8530. That title matches Alabama's statewide crib. Nearby counties use different titles: Madison uses Tax Assessor and Shelby uses Property Tax Commissioner. Mobile's own pages describe H-1 through H-4. H-3 for age 65 and older with federal taxable income of $12,000 or less is a total exemption from state, county and city taxes. H-2 and H-3 based on age or income, plus disability and blindness exemptions, must be reclaimed every year by December 31. H-3 owners get a renewal form in October in lieu of a tax bill. Miss December 31 and the exemption cancels."
  - question: "Is there a PACE program in Mobile County, AL?"
    answer: "Yes. Alabama Medicaid limits the PACE service area to Mobile and Baldwin counties, and Mercy LIFE of Alabama operates the program from 2900 Springhill Avenue, Mobile, AL 36607. Call 251-287-8420, or 855-287-8427 for referrals. Eligibility requires age 55 or older, nursing-home level of care, the ability to live safely in the community at enrollment, and residence in the designated service area. If PACE is not the right fit, ask the Area Agency on Aging at 251-706-4680 about the Medicaid Elderly and Disabled Waiver."
  - question: "Who helps a senior in Mobile pay the power bill?"
    answer: "Start with Mobile Community Action, which runs both LIHEAP and weatherization for Mobile County from 461 Donald Street, Mobile, AL 36617. The main line is 251-457-5700. LIHEAP appointments go through 251-206-6117 or the online scheduler on mcamobile.org. Heating season runs January 1 through April 30 and cooling season runs June 1 through September 30. Weatherization uses a 200 percent of poverty income ceiling and prioritizes elderly households, people with disabilities and families with children. For Alabama Power customers, ask about Project Share through the local Salvation Army or 2-1-1, because the intake phone can change with funding."
  - question: "How does an older adult in Mobile County get meals?"
    answer: "Call the Area Agency on Aging at 251-706-4680. Aging South Alabama funds senior nutrition centers across Mobile County and offers frozen home-delivered meals where centers do not deliver. Centers with published phones include Bayou La Batre at 251-824-4616, Citronelle at 251-866-5047, Prichard at 251-452-7918, Saraland at 251-679-5511 extension 3, Dumas Wesley at 251-479-0649 and Tillman's Corner at 251-661-6600. Call the center closest to you before driving. For SNAP, ask Mobile County DHR for the Alabama Elderly Simplified Application Project by name. The current state form, revised October 2025, is for households where all members are 65 or older. State help line: 1-800-438-2958."
  - question: "Can a Mobile County senior who has stopped driving still get around?"
    answer: "Inside Wave Transit's service area, yes. The fixed-route fare is $1.25, and seniors 65 and older ride for $0.60 with proper ID after registering at the GM&O Transportation Center, 110 Beauregard Street, Suite 104. Customer service is 251-344-6600. For riders whose disability prevents use of the fixed route, the Mobility Assistance Program, or MAP, is curb-to-curb paratransit at $2.50 per one-way trip with a prepaid ticket. Reserve at 251-344-5656 from one day to 14 days ahead. Wave does not run fixed routes on Sundays. Outside the Wave footprint, call the Area Agency on Aging at 251-706-4680 or 2-1-1."
  - question: "My mother has dementia and wanders. What is available in Mobile County?"
    answer: "Project Lifesaver through the Mobile County Sheriff's Office is free. Participants wear a wristband transmitter that deputies can locate with specialized receivers. Contact Casey Fields at projectlifesaver@mobileso.com or 251-402-6811. The Sheriff's Office is at 510 South Royal Street, Mobile, AL 36603, main line 251-574-2423. Separately, Alabama Cares caregiver support and respite run through the Area Agency on Aging at 251-706-4680. The Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline is 1-800-272-3900."
  - question: "Is there help with home repairs for a senior homeowner in Mobile County?"
    answer: "Inside the City of Mobile, start with the Neighborhood Development Critical Repair Grant Program at 251-208-6294. The 2026 cycle focused on roofs and took applications in person at the Levon C. Manzie Neighborhood Resource Center. Call before assuming a window is open. Countywide, Mobile Community Action runs weatherization at 251-457-5700 with a 200 percent of poverty income ceiling. Habitat for Humanity of Southwest Alabama has published that repair program applications are closed, so check habitatswalabama.org or call before driving. Outside those doors, 2-1-1 tracks which church and volunteer repair programs currently have capacity."
