*Free, plain-English help for seniors and families in Sarasota County, FL. Education, not advice. Verify before you rely on it.*

## Start here (works anywhere)
- 211 (dial 2-1-1), Eldercare Locator 1-800-677-1116, BenefitsCheckUp.org, Medicare 1-800-633-4227, Social Security 1-800-772-1213, VA 1-800-827-1000, Alzheimer's 24/7 Helpline 1-800-272-3900, National Elder Fraud Hotline 1-833-372-8311, Crisis Lifeline 988

**Three local front doors:** the **Area Agency on Aging for Southwest Florida**, whose **Elder Helpline is 1-866-413-5337** (1-866-41-ELDER), Monday through Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., main office 2830 Winkler Ave., Suite 112, Fort Myers, FL 33916, 239-652-6900 | **Senior Friendship Centers**, at **1888 Brother Geenen Way, Sarasota, FL 34236, 941-955-2122** and **2350 Scenic Drive, Venice, FL 34293, 941-584-0052** | **211 Suncoast Cares**, serving Sarasota, Manatee, and DeSoto counties (dial **2-1-1**, or **text your ZIP code to 898-211**)

Two of those three deserve a note before you start dialing. The **Area Agency on Aging for Southwest Florida** is headquartered in Fort Myers and covers seven counties, so a Sarasota family sometimes hesitates to call a number that is not local. Call it anyway. It is the designated Aging and Disability Resource Center for this county, and it is the office that determines eligibility for home delivered meals, runs SHINE Medicare counseling, holds the caregiver programs, and starts the Medicaid long-term care process. Almost every program below eventually routes back through **1-866-413-5337**.

**Senior Friendship Centers** is the local agency that actually does things. It runs the dining cafés, the adult day program, in-home case management, telephone reassurance, help filling out ACCESS benefit applications, free legal consultations, and the elder energy program. One change worth knowing before you visit: the **Activity Center now runs on membership**, at $190 a year for an individual, $120 each for a couple, or $130 for six months, and all center programs are free once you are a member. **Partial and full scholarships are available**, and you ask the Center Manager. Do not let the price tag stop a parent from walking in. The meal program and the case management side are separate from that membership.

Sarasota County's own contact center is **311 or 941-861-5000**, TTY **7-1-1 or 1-800-955-8771**. The county also employs an **Aging System Coordinator, Susan Berger, at 941-861-2558**, sberger@scgov.net, which is an unusual position for a county to fund and a useful person to reach when you cannot find the right agency.

There is a reason for that position. **In 2015 Sarasota County became Florida's first member of the World Health Organization's Global Network of Age-Friendly Cities and Communities**, and of the AARP Network of Age-Friendly Communities. In 2019 the county and the Florida Department of Health took over leadership of the initiative, and the Seniors Advisory Council, which has existed since 2000, reviews the work. That history is why this county has more senior infrastructure than its size would predict.

Two more numbers to write down. The **Florida Abuse Hotline** at **1-800-962-2873** takes reports of abuse, neglect, exploitation, or self-neglect of a vulnerable adult, 24 hours a day, with TTY at 711 or 1-800-955-8771. Press 2 for the vulnerable adult line. And the **Long-Term Care Ombudsman at 1-888-831-0404** is who you call about a nursing home or assisted living facility, not the facility's own management.

**Before hurricane season, do this one thing.** Sarasota County Emergency Management keeps a **Medical Needs Program** registry for residents who qualify as a medically dependent person, and pre-registering is what gets you on the list for **transportation and sheltering at a medically dependent evacuation center**. Register through the Florida Special Needs Registry or call **311 or 941-861-5000**. Read the rules before you assume you are covered: **every registrant must bring a caregiver** who can help with dressing and toileting, lift the person to and from a cot or wheelchair, assist with walking, carry food from the cafeteria, and manage medications and oxygen. County transportation is limited to the registrant, the caregiver, a spouse, and underage children, and **registered residents are called 24 to 48 hours before high winds arrive** to ask whether they need a ride. If you say no then and change your mind later, transportation is not guaranteed.

## Property tax relief (apply with the Sarasota County Property Appraiser, 941-861-8200, 2001 Adams Lane)
Florida seniors apply for property tax relief through the county Property Appraiser, not the Tax Collector. In Sarasota County that is **Bill Furst**, main office **2001 Adams Lane, Sarasota, FL 34237**, phone **941-861-8200**, Monday through Friday 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. There are two branch offices: **4000 S. Tamiami Trail, Room 1100, Venice, FL 34293** and **4970 City Hall Blvd., Room 128a, North Port, FL 34286**.

**Standard Homestead Exemption (statewide):** you must be a Florida resident who owns and occupies the property as a permanent residence **on January 1** of the year you are claiming, and the filing deadline is **March 1**. After the first year, the annual increase in assessed value is capped, and that cap is **Save Our Homes**, which over a decade is usually worth more than any exemption below.

**Now the senior exemption, and Sarasota County is the clearest example in Florida of why the number on a state website is not the number on your bill.** Florida law lets each county and each city decide, by local ordinance, whether to grant an additional homestead exemption to low-income seniors and how much it is worth, up to $50,000. The exemption applies **only to the millage of the government that adopted it**. In Sarasota County, five different governments made five different decisions, and the Property Appraiser publishes them plainly:

- **$50,000** against **City of Sarasota** municipal taxes
- **$50,000** against **City of Venice** municipal taxes
- **$50,000** against **Town of Longboat Key** municipal taxes
- **$25,000** against **City of North Port** municipal taxes
- **$5,000** against **Sarasota County** taxes

Read that last line twice. **If your parent lives in unincorporated Sarasota County, which is most of the county's land and a large share of its housing, the $5,000 county exemption is the only senior exemption they get.** If they live inside the City of Sarasota, Venice, or Longboat Key, they get the county's $5,000 against the county levy **and** $50,000 against the city levy. Two neighbors a mile apart, same age, same income, can have completely different results. Nobody at the state level will tell you this. Give the Property Appraiser the exact street address and ask which taxing authorities on that specific bill have adopted it.

**And here is what this page will not overstate.** Florida authorizes a **second** senior exemption for owners who have kept permanent residence for at least 25 years on a property with just value under $250,000, which can wipe out the adopting government's entire levy. Several Florida counties have adopted it. **Sarasota County's Property Appraiser lists only the limited-income version**, with no 25-year long-term residency exemption among the ordinances it publishes. If a neighbor in another county mentions the 25-year break, that is why it does not appear on your bill. Ask anyway when you call, because ordinances change.

**The requirements and the paperwork.** You must already have a current homestead exemption, **at least one owner must be 65 or older as of January 1** of the filing year, and **total household adjusted gross income must not exceed $38,686 for 2026**. That figure is set annually by the state and was $37,694 for 2025. The Property Appraiser is specific about the income test: it is the amount on **Line 11 of Form 1040 or 1040-SR**, and it is the adjusted gross income of **all members of the household**, not just the person who qualifies by age, including spouses, partners, and other relatives. Proof of age can be a Florida driver license, a Florida ID card, a birth certificate, or another certified record.

Two deadlines, not one, and the second is the one people miss. The **Sworn Statement of Adjusted Gross Income of Household, form DR-501SC, is due to the Property Appraiser by March 1**. Then, if you or anyone else living on the property files a federal income tax return for the previous year, **a copy of each filed return must be submitted by June 1**. Missing that June date after clearing the March one is a real way to lose the exemption.

One more limit worth knowing so the savings do not disappoint. **School, Hospital, West Coast Inland Navigation District, and Southwest Florida Water Management District taxes are not eligible** for this exemption. School taxes are usually the largest single line on a Florida bill, and this exemption does not touch them.

**A separate office handles the bill.** The **Sarasota County Tax Collector**, **941-861-8300**, Info@SarasotaTaxCollector.gov, cannot grant exemptions but does run the move that helps most on a fixed income: the **quarterly installment plan**. If the previous year's taxes were **more than $100**, you can split the bill into four payments by applying **between November 1 and April 30**. The first installment is due **June 30** with a **6 percent discount**, the second **September 30** at **4.5 percent**, the third **December 31** at **3 percent**, and the fourth **March 31** with no discount. **Failure to make the first payment automatically cancels you from the plan** for that year, and the whole bill comes due by March 31 instead. Once you are in, you are **re-enrolled automatically** each year with no new application.

## Food
- **Meals on Wheels of Sarasota**, **421 N. Lime Ave., Sarasota, FL 34237**, **941-366-6693**, info@mowsrq.org, delivers a meal **Monday through Friday**. Eligibility is **60 or older**, plus being homebound or having a medical condition that limits cooking. Meals are **$8 each with a reduced rate based on household income**, and the organization states it has **no waiting list**, which is rare and worth acting on.
- **Check the boundary before you call, because it is not the county line.** Meals on Wheels of Sarasota serves **northern Sarasota County only: from the Gulf of Mexico east to the county line, and from University Parkway in the north to Laurel Road in the south**. **Venice, North Port, and Englewood are outside that area.** If a parent lives south of Laurel Road, this is the wrong number and nobody will necessarily tell you that on the first call.
- **For the rest of the county, and as a second option anywhere in it, Senior Friendship Centers runs Friendship Home Delivered Meals** for people **60 and older**, delivered **once a week** as usually up to **seven frozen meals** at a time. Eligibility is determined through the **Elder Helpline at 1-866-413-5337**. Senior Friendship Centers serves Sarasota, DeSoto, Charlotte, and Lee counties and puts out close to 300,000 meals a year.
- **The Friendship Café is the underused half of the food answer.** Congregate dining for the **60 and over** crowd runs **Monday through Friday, 11:45 a.m. to 12:15 p.m. in Sarasota** and **12:00 to 12:30 p.m. in Venice**. There is a **suggested donation**, and **no one is denied a meal for inability to pay**. Call **941-955-2122**. Getting a parent out of the house five days a week has its own value beyond the plate.
- **All Faiths Food Bank**, **8171 Blaikie Court, Sarasota, FL 34240**, **941-379-6333**, is the regional food bank for **Sarasota and DeSoto counties**, distributing 20.4 million meals last year and running roughly **870 grocery pickup opportunities each month**. Use the **Food Finder** tool on its site or ask for a printable distribution schedule.
- **The Salvation Army food pantry in Venice**, **1051 Albee Farm Road, Venice, FL 34285**, **941-484-6227**, is open **Mondays and Thursdays, 9:30 to 11:30 a.m.**, and provides a three-to-five-day supply of groceries free of charge. Bring a valid photo ID. It is first come, first served.
- **SNAP** (food stamps) is based on income, not age. Apply any time through Florida's MyACCESS portal at **myaccess.myflfamilies.com** or call **850-300-4323**. **Senior Friendship Centers will help you fill out the ACCESS application** in Sarasota and Venice.

## Energy and utility help
Sarasota County splits energy help by age, and the agency that sounds most obvious is the wrong one. Read this section before you make a call.

- **EHEAP is the 60 and older program.** The Florida Department of Elder Affairs 2025-2026 provider list names **Senior Friendship Centers** as the EHEAP provider for Sarasota County, at **1888 Brother Geenen Way, Sarasota, FL 34236**, with an EHEAP line of **941-271-9800** and intake at **eheapsrq@friendshipcenters.org**. The lead agency for the region is the Area Agency on Aging for Southwest Florida, and **FPL's own assistance page lists the Elder Helpline, 1-866-413-5337, as the EHEAP contact for Sarasota County**. Either number gets you into the same program. EHEAP requires at least one person **60 or older** in the household, household gross income at or below **60 percent of State Median Income**, and a **verifiable home energy crisis**, meaning a delinquent utility bill, a shut-off notice, or a lack of fuel or wood. Eligible households **may receive more than one crisis benefit per program year**, up to a cap set by FloridaCommerce. Ask the office for the current cap rather than trusting a figure you read elsewhere, because no per-county dollar amount is published.
- **LIHEAP is the general program, and in Sarasota County it runs through the Salvation Army.** FPL's assistance-by-county page lists the Salvation Army for Sarasota LIHEAP at **941-954-4673**, **941-364-8845**, and **941-484-6227**. The Salvation Army Sarasota County main office is **1400 10th Street, Sarasota, FL 34236**. Applications are normally filed through **floridaliheap.com/relief_program**. Be aware that the state portal has periods when it posts that **applications are not currently being accepted**, so if you hit that message, call the local office rather than assuming the program is gone.
- **Here is the trap.** **Step Up Suncoast has a Sarasota mailing address, at 6428 Parkland Drive, Sarasota, FL 34243, but its LIHEAP service area is Manatee and Hardee counties, not Sarasota.** Families call it first because of the address and lose a week. Step Up Suncoast **is** the right call for something else, which is the next bullet.
- **Weatherization is the permanent fix, and Step Up Suncoast runs it for Charlotte, Manatee, and Sarasota counties.** Call **941-827-2887 ext. 7806**, the programs line at **941-827-0188**, or toll-free **855-827-5081**. The work covers health and safety measures such as detectors and exhaust fans, insulation, lighting retrofits, low-flow fixtures, weather stripping and duct sealing, solar window screens, **refrigerator replacement**, and **HVAC system replacement**. It is income qualified, but the income figures posted on the program page carry a **July 1, 2021** effective date, so ask for the current limits rather than ruling yourself out on a stale number.
- **FPL Care To Share** is administered locally by the Salvation Army at **941-556-0234**. It is a hardship fund, separate from LIHEAP, and worth asking about in the same call.
- For a past-due bill right now, dial **2-1-1** or text your ZIP code to **898-211**. They keep the current list of church ministries and local funds that still have utility money this month.

## Medicare, Medicaid, prescriptions
- Florida's SHIP, the free Medicare counseling program, is **SHINE, Serving Health Insurance Needs of Elders**, and in this region it is delivered by the **Area Agency on Aging for Southwest Florida**. Counseling is **free** and the counselors are volunteers who do not sell insurance. They handle turning 65, billing problems, plan comparisons, and appeals. Call **1-866-413-5337**. Start here before any Medicare decision and especially before switching plans during open enrollment. The statewide program site is floridashine.org.
- **SMP, the Senior Medicare Patrol**, is the fraud side of the same house, helping beneficiaries and families detect and report health care fraud, errors, and abuse. Report suspected Medicare fraud through the statewide Elder Helpline at **1-800-963-5337**, which is 1-800-96-ELDER.
- **Medicaid** in Florida, including long-term care coverage and the programs that help pay Medicare costs, is applied for through the Department of Children and Families at **myaccess.myflfamilies.com** or **850-300-4323**. The same single application covers SNAP food assistance and Temporary Cash Assistance.
- **Medicare Savings Programs** and **Extra Help**, the Low-Income Subsidy that lowers Part D drug costs, are screened through SHINE (1-866-413-5337), Social Security (1-800-772-1213), and MyACCESS. Florida does not run a general statewide senior drug program, so ask SHINE about Extra Help and use BenefitsCheckUp.org to find manufacturer and nonprofit patient-assistance programs for specific medications.
- **Senior Friendship Centers helps people complete ACCESS applications** for food assistance, cash assistance, and Medicaid in both Sarasota and Venice. Call **941-955-2122** or **941-584-0052**. Sitting with someone while you fill out MyACCESS is often the difference between an approval and an abandoned application.

## Home repair and safety
Sarasota County's repair money runs on a calendar, and the calendar is about to turn.

- **The State Housing Initiatives Partnership (SHIP) is the main door**, administered by the county's **Housing Enhancement and Redevelopment Office (HERO)**, **4510 Fruitville Rd, 2nd Floor, Sarasota, FL 34232**, by appointment only. Call **941-861-5345** or the case manager at **941-861-5336**, or email **SHIP@scgov.net**. Applications run through herosrq.net/ship.
- **The money is a zero percent interest deferred loan, forgiven at 5 percent per year over 20 years.** That is the detail that changes the decision for an older homeowner. A person who stays in the home does not repay it, and it is not a monthly payment.
- **Owner-Occupied Rehabilitation** covers repairs that address substandard conditions, code violations, and major system upgrades. The county also runs **Purchase Assistance** and the **ARCH** program for acquisition and rehabilitation.
- **Applications for 2025-2026 are closed, and the next cycle is scheduled to open September 1, 2026.** The county has said it will provide application help at two library locations during September and October 2026. If a repair is not an emergency, this is worth waiting a few weeks for.
- **The shared rules:** the applicant must own the home, it must be their **primary residence**, it must be **in Sarasota County**, and the **mortgage and property taxes must be current**.
- **Step Up Suncoast's Weatherization program** at **941-827-2887** is the other real door, and it can replace an HVAC system or a refrigerator without any of the SHIP calendar.
- **2-1-1** can find a faith-based or volunteer group that does small safety work such as grab bars and ramps when no government program is open.
- **One warning that belongs in a home repair section.** Sarasota County has approved several **Property Assessed Clean Energy (PACE)** providers, which finance impact windows, roofs, air conditioning, and solar and repay the loan as an assessment **on your property tax bill**. The county's own page states plainly that PACE financing is **neither offered nor endorsed by Sarasota County**, that it **comes with significant risks that must be understood fully**, and that owners should get multiple quotes on both the improvements and the financing. Older homeowners are the population most often sold these at the door. Read that page before signing anything, and do not confuse this PACE with the elder care program of the same acronym further down.

## Rent, housing, emergency cash
- **Season of Sharing is the big local one, and this county is where it started.** Administered by the **Community Foundation of Sarasota County**, it serves **Sarasota, Manatee, Charlotte, and DeSoto** counties and covers **rent or mortgage, utility bills, childcare, transportation, and other crucial expenses**. Two things make it work well for older adults: **funds go directly to the landlord, utility, or provider**, never as cash to the household, and you do not apply to the foundation at all.
- **How you actually reach it: call 2-1-1**, or go through one of the partner nonprofits in its network. The 211 specialist refers you to the partner agency that fits your situation and location. The Community Foundation's own line for questions about the program is **941-955-3000**.
- **The Salvation Army** provides emergency financial assistance toward **utility bills and rent or mortgage payments** in Sarasota County, subject to funding availability. Main office **1400 10th Street, Sarasota, FL 34236**.
- **211 Suncoast Cares** is the live directory of who still has money this month. Dial **2-1-1** or **text your ZIP code to 898-211**. Help is free and confidential and available in multiple languages.
- For Medicaid, SNAP, and Temporary Cash Assistance, apply through MyACCESS at **myaccess.myflfamilies.com** or **850-300-4323**.

## Transportation
**Start with the fact that surprises people: on Sarasota County's Breeze buses, riders 80 and over ride free.** Not discounted, free. You show ID to the driver or to an agent at a ticket outlet. That is unusual, and it is worth checking a parent's birthday against it.

The rest of the fare table is short. The **standard fare is $1.50** on the bus. **Half fare, 75 cents**, applies to **seniors 65 and older** with ID, to **Medicare cardholders** with ID plus the Medicare card, and to people with a qualifying disability who submit a Certification of Physical Impairment. A **30-day pass is $50** and a reloadable Smart Card is $4. **Route 99 is free to everyone**, because FDOT fully funds its operation and the County Commission passed the savings on. Standard routes run **5 a.m. to 11 p.m. Monday through Saturday** and **6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Sunday**. Since **April 25, 2026** you can buy mobile tickets through the **Token Transit** app, and overpayments in cash come back as a Change Voucher rather than change.

**If the regular bus will not work, that is Breeze Plus, and its eligibility rules are more generous than most counties.** Breeze Plus covers three programs and a rider may qualify for more than one.
- **The Transportation Disadvantaged (TD) program is the one to know about.** You qualify if you are **already approved under the ADA program, or aged 65 or older, or financially burdened with household income below 150 percent of the federal poverty level**. Age alone is a door here, with no disability required. The **TD service area is all of Sarasota County**, and service hours are **Monday through Saturday, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.** Trips are prioritized by purpose: critical care first, then other medical, then employment, education, and other.
- **The ADA program** serves people whose disability prevents them from independently using the fixed route service, and it is curb to curb, with pickup and drop-off **within three quarters of a mile of a Breeze route**. Hours are Monday through Saturday 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. and Sunday 6:20 a.m. to 10:30 p.m.
- **Applying:** complete the Eligibility-based Program Application, with disabilities documented by a qualified medical professional. Submit by email to **eligibility@scgov.net**, by mail to Mobility Coordinator, 5303 Pinkney Ave, Sarasota, FL 34233, or by fax to 941-861-1007. Questions about eligibility or application status go to **941-861-1018**. Start this before you need it.
- **Fares and booking:** a one-way Breeze Plus trip is **$3**. ## Legal help
- **Gulfcoast Legal Services is the first call, and the reason is a funding rule most families never hear.** GLS generally considers representation for clients under **250 percent of the federal poverty level**, but it also states that its **VOCA funding provides services for all victims of crime regardless of income, and funding for clients aged 60 and older**. So a retired homeowner with savings is not automatically disqualified. The **Sarasota office is at 1750 17th Street, Building C-3, Sarasota, FL 34234**, reachable at **1-800-230-5920**. GLS covers Pinellas, Manatee, and Sarasota counties and handles family law and domestic violence, financial stability, housing stability, immigration, plus consumer, elder law, and public benefits matters. Apply online any time, or walk in **Monday through Friday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.**, noting the office closes 12:30 to 1:30 Monday through Thursday and takes no walk-ins after 12:30 on Fridays.
- **Legal Aid of Manasota** is the other local provider, serving **Sarasota and Manatee counties**, with offices at **1900 Main Street, Suite 302, Sarasota, FL 34236, 941-366-0038** and **749 Shamrock Boulevard, Venice, FL 34293, 941-492-4631**, both Monday through Friday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Its hotline is **1-800-625-2257**. It handles matters involving homes, health, and family, plus veterans benefits.
- **Florida's Senior Legal Helpline** gives free legal advice and brief services by phone to Florida residents **60 and older** on civil matters including housing, family, government benefits, and contract and consumer problems. Call **1-888-895-7873**, Monday through Friday 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. It is operated by Bay Area Legal Services and serves seniors statewide, so it is a second shot if both local offices are at capacity. Interpreters are free.
- **Senior Friendship Centers offers free 30-minute legal consultations by appointment** at both its Sarasota and Venice centers. Call **941-955-2122** or **941-584-0052**. For a simple question, that is faster than an intake queue.
- To report suspected abuse, neglect, self-neglect, or exploitation of a vulnerable adult, call the **Florida Abuse Hotline at 1-800-962-2873**, 24 hours a day, and press 2. For a problem inside a nursing home or assisted living facility, call the **Long-Term Care Ombudsman at 1-888-831-0404**.

## Caregiver support
- **The Caregiving Place is the centerpiece here, and there are two of them.** Senior Friendship Centers runs adult day programs at **1820 Brother Geenen Way, Sarasota** and **2350 Scenic Drive, Venice**, both reachable at **941-556-3268**, open **Monday through Friday 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.** They are built for people with **dementia or other cognitive challenges**, with music, art and pet therapy, cognitive stimulation, and outdoor space.
- **Two details make it usable.** The staffing ratio is **1 to 6**, and there are **half-day options**, either **8 a.m. to noon or noon to 4 p.m.**, alongside the full day. A caregiver who cannot afford or does not need a full day can still get four hours back. **Continental breakfast and a hot lunch** are included. Ask about fee assistance when you call rather than assuming the cost.
- **The Caregiver Resource Centers at both sites** run support groups, classes, reference materials, and a lending library, and they are open to caregivers whether or not the person they care for attends the day program.
- **The Area Agency on Aging for Southwest Florida carries the structured programs**, all reachable at **1-866-413-5337**. **TCARE**, the Tailored Caregiver Assessment and Referral program, works through the actual stressors rather than handing over a brochure. **Powerful Tools for Caregivers** is a six-session evidence-based class on self-care and stress management. **Savvy Caregiver** is a separate six-session program built specifically for people caring for someone with Alzheimer's or another dementia. The agency also offers a free caregiver platform through a partnership with Caregiving.com.
- **Friendship at Home** is the quiet one that matters for isolation. Senior Friendship Centers volunteers make **weekly telephone reassurance calls**, run a **friendly visitor program** for companionship and errands, and provide supportive intervention including limited financial crisis assistance. For Sarasota and Venice, call **941-584-0050**.
- **For diagnosis, Sarasota County has its own state-designated memory clinic, which not every Florida county does.** Florida's legislature funds a network of **Memory Disorder Clinics** providing comprehensive diagnostic and referral services, and the one serving this area is the **Sarasota Memorial Hospital Memory Disorder Clinic, 1515 S. Osprey Ave., Suite A-1, Sarasota, FL 34239, 941-917-7197**. If you are chasing a diagnosis and getting nowhere, this is the door.
- The **Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline** (**1-800-272-3900**) is staffed at any hour, and 211 can point you to local support groups.

## Staying at home instead of a nursing home
- Florida Medicaid pays for care at home through the **Statewide Medicaid Managed Care Long-Term Care** program, for people **65 and older who are Medicaid eligible**, or **18 and older and Medicaid eligible by reason of a disability**, who are also determined by the state's **CARES** unit to meet a **nursing home level of care**. Plans must cover at minimum a long list that includes adult companion care, adult day care, assisted living facility services, attendant care, caregiver training, case management, home accessibility adaptation, home delivered meals, homemaker services, hospice, skilled nursing, medical equipment and supplies, nursing facility care, occupational, physical, respiratory and speech therapy, personal care, personal emergency response systems, respite care, and transportation. **Start with the Elder Helpline at 1-866-413-5337**, ask about the waitlist on that first call, and start early. Plan choices and choice counseling are at flmedicaidmanagedcare.com.
- **PACE, the Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly**, wraps medical care, therapy, an adult day center, home care, and prescriptions into one program for people **55 and older** who meet a nursing home level of care but can live at home with help. **Sarasota County has one PACE organization assigned to it, and you should know its numbers before you get your hopes up.**
- **Florida's Agency for Health Care Administration** monthly PACE report for **February 2026** lists Sarasota County with **Stratum PACE, status Operational, zero enrollees present for the current month and zero unique individuals served in the fiscal year to date**. The report's own footnote explains what a zero means: the organization covers the county, but no participants from that county are enrolled in it. Across its entire service area of **Manatee, Sarasota, and DeSoto**, the organization had **18 enrollees** against **150 slots approved and 150 funded**, with initial AHCA approval on **March 14, 2021**. No other PACE organization is listed as present, pending, or under review for Sarasota County.
- **The program now operates as Empath LIFE**, which is why searching the AHCA name gets you nowhere. The center serving **Manatee and Sarasota counties** is at **303 13th Avenue E., Bradenton, FL 34208**, and the enrollment line is **1-866-458-2933**, with 1-800-955-8771 for hearing impaired callers. The Florida PACE Association also lists the program at **941-248-1550**. Eligibility is being **at least 55**, living in the **Manatee or Sarasota** service area, meeting **nursing home level of care**, and being able to remain safely at home with the center's help.
- **So what should a family actually do with that?** Call, because a program with open slots and almost nobody in them has capacity. But do not build the plan around it. Run the **Medicaid long-term care** track through the Elder Helpline at the same time, and note that the day center is in Bradenton, which is a real commute from Venice or North Port.
- **Do not confuse this with the other PACE.** Sarasota County's own website has a prominent page for **Property Assessed Clean Energy**, a home improvement financing program repaid through your property tax bill. Same four letters, entirely different thing, and the county explicitly warns that it carries significant risk. If someone knocks on a senior's door talking about PACE, find out which one they mean before anyone signs.
- The county's own senior infrastructure also helps someone stay home longer without any Medicaid determination: **Senior Friendship Centers in-home case management, telephone reassurance, adult day services, and meals**, all starting at **941-955-2122**, and the county's **Aging System Coordinator at 941-861-2558** when you cannot find the right agency.

## Frequently asked questions
**Mom is 78 and owns a modest house east of I-75 in unincorporated Sarasota County. How much is the senior exemption actually worth to her?** Less than she has probably been told, and this is the single most misunderstood fact in the county. The Limited Income Senior Exemption is a **local option**, and each government set its own amount. **Sarasota County adopted $5,000**, so against the county's portion of her bill that is what she gets, assuming she is **65 or older as of January 1**, already has homestead, and household adjusted gross income is at or below **$38,686**. If that same house were inside the **City of Sarasota, City of Venice, or Town of Longboat Key**, she would also get **$50,000** against the municipal levy, and **$25,000** in the **City of North Port**. She should still file. File the **DR-501SC by March 1** with the Property Appraiser at **941-861-8200**, and send copies of any filed federal returns by **June 1**. And note that school, hospital, WCIND, and SWFWMD taxes are not touched by any of it.

**I keep reading about a Florida exemption for people who have owned their home 25 years. Can my father get it in Sarasota?** Probably not here, and it is worth knowing why so you stop chasing it. Florida authorizes **two** additional senior exemptions, and the second one, for owners 65 and older who have kept permanent residence at least **25 years** on a property with **just value under $250,000**, can wipe out the adopting government's entire levy. But it only exists where a county or city passed an ordinance adopting it, and **Sarasota County's Property Appraiser publishes only the limited-income version**. Ask when you call **941-861-8200**, because ordinances do change, but do not plan around it.

**Dad is homebound in Venice and cannot cook. Who brings him meals?** Not Meals on Wheels of Sarasota, and that is the trap. Its service area stops at **Laurel Road**, so Venice, North Port, and Englewood are outside it. Call the **Elder Helpline at 1-866-413-5337** and ask to be assessed for **Friendship Home Delivered Meals** through Senior Friendship Centers, which delivers up to seven frozen meals once a week to people 60 and older. Also ask about the **Venice Friendship Café** at **2350 Scenic Drive, 941-584-0052**, which serves lunch Monday through Friday from noon to 12:30 for a suggested donation, with no one turned away for inability to pay. If he lives north of Laurel Road instead, then **Meals on Wheels of Sarasota at 941-366-6693** is the right call, and they say they have no waiting list.

**The power bill is past due and my mother is 82 in North Port. Who do I call first?** Because she is over 60, start with **EHEAP**. The state lists **Senior Friendship Centers** as Sarasota County's EHEAP provider, at **941-271-9800** or eheapsrq@friendshipcenters.org, and you can also reach the program through the **Elder Helpline at 1-866-413-5337**, which is the number FPL publishes for it. EHEAP needs a documented crisis such as a shut-off notice, and a household can receive more than one crisis benefit in a program year. In parallel, ask about **LIHEAP through the Salvation Army**, which FPL lists for Sarasota County at **941-954-4673** and **941-364-8845**, and about **FPL Care To Share at 941-556-0234**. **Do not call Step Up Suncoast for LIHEAP**, even though its office address says Sarasota, because its LIHEAP area is Manatee and Hardee. Call Step Up Suncoast at **941-827-2887** for **weatherization** instead, which can replace an aging air conditioner rather than just paying one bill.

**My father stopped driving. What does it cost him to get around Sarasota County?** If he is **80 or older and can ride a regular bus, nothing.** Breeze waives the fare entirely at 80 with ID. Between **65 and 79** it is **75 cents** a ride against a standard fare of $1.50, and Route 99 is free to everyone. If a regular bus will not work, apply for **Breeze Plus**, and here is the part most families miss: the **Transportation Disadvantaged program accepts anyone 65 or older**, with no disability required, and it covers **all of Sarasota County**. Apply by emailing **eligibility@scgov.net** or calling **941-861-1018**, then book rides at **941-777-6000** at least a day ahead. A Breeze Plus trip is **$3** each way, or **$1.50** inside a Breeze OnDemand zone, and a registered personal care attendant rides free.

**Mom needs a lawyer about a contractor who took her money, but she owns her home outright. Does she make too much for legal aid?** Quite possibly not. **Gulfcoast Legal Services** generally uses a 250 percent of poverty test, but it states that its **VOCA funding covers all victims of crime regardless of income, and clients aged 60 and older**. Call the Sarasota office at **1-800-230-5920** or go to 1750 17th Street, Building C-3. If they cannot take it, try **Legal Aid of Manasota** at **941-366-0038** in Sarasota or **941-492-4631** in Venice, or the statewide **Florida Senior Legal Helpline at 1-888-895-7873**, which is free to any Florida resident 60 and older. And for a quick question, **Senior Friendship Centers offers a free 30-minute legal consultation by appointment** at both centers.

**Is the PACE program in Sarasota County real?** It is real, and it is nearly empty, and both facts matter. Florida AHCA's **February 2026** report lists **Stratum PACE** as **Operational** for Sarasota County with **zero enrollees** that month and **zero** unique individuals served in the fiscal year to date. Across its whole Manatee, Sarasota, and DeSoto service area it had **18 enrollees against 150 funded slots**. The program now goes by **Empath LIFE**, and the center serving Sarasota is in **Bradenton at 303 13th Avenue E.**, reachable at **1-866-458-2933**. Call, because capacity is clearly there, but start the **Medicaid long-term care** conversation with the **Elder Helpline at 1-866-413-5337** in parallel, and factor in the drive from south county. Separately, if your search results are full of solar panels and impact windows, you have found **Property Assessed Clean Energy**, a loan repaid on the property tax bill that the county itself warns carries significant risk. It is not the same program.

**My husband has dementia and I need a few hours back. What is available in Sarasota County?** Three things, in this order. Call **The Caregiving Place at 941-556-3268**, which runs adult day programs for people with dementia in **Sarasota at 1820 Brother Geenen Way** and **Venice at 2350 Scenic Drive**, Monday through Friday, with a **1 to 6 staffing ratio** and **half-day options** of 8 a.m. to noon or noon to 4 p.m. Second, call the **Area Agency on Aging at 1-866-413-5337** and ask about **TCARE** and about **Savvy Caregiver**, the six-session program built specifically for dementia caregivers. Third, if the diagnosis itself is unclear, **Sarasota Memorial Hospital's Memory Disorder Clinic at 941-917-7197** is one of Florida's state-designated memory clinics and is right here in the county. The **Alzheimer's Association helpline at 1-800-272-3900** is answered at 3 a.m., which is often when you need it.
