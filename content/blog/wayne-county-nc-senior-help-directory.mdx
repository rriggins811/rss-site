## Property tax relief (apply with the Wayne County Tax Department, 919-731-1461)

**The office.** Senior and disability relief is handled by the **Wayne County Tax Department**, **224 E. Walnut Street, Goldsboro, NC 27530** (mail to **P.O. Box 227, Goldsboro**), **Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.** The **Tax Administrator is Alan Lumpkin**, whom the state's official list of county assessors (revised September 23, 2026) names as Wayne's County Assessor at **919-731-1461**, the department's **listing** line. Other lines: **Tax Collections 919-731-1478**, **Tax Mapping 919-731-1585**. Payments by mail go to a different box: **Wayne County Tax Collector, P.O. Box 1495, Goldsboro, NC 27533**.

**The three programs.** All three are set by the state and filed on the state's **Form AV-9** with the county. The county posts the AV-9 and the veterans' form on its Tax Forms page.

- **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion.** It **excludes the greater of $25,000 or 50 percent of the appraised value of the home**, and the county notes that includes **mobile homes**. You must be **65 or older, or totally and permanently disabled** (the county asks for a licensed physician's certification), and own and live in the home. Income for the prior year must be within the state limit: **$38,800 for the 2026 tax year, $39,900 for 2027**. The county's relief page still prints last year's **$37,900** figure; the state's 2026 and 2027 forms are the current numbers. For a married couple living together, **both incomes count**.
- **Circuit Breaker Tax Deferment.** Caps the tax at **4 percent of income up to $38,800** and **5 percent from $38,801 to $58,200** for 2026 (for 2027 the ceilings are **$39,900** and **$59,850**). The owner must be **65 or older or totally and permanently disabled** and must have **owned and lived in the home at least five years**. The county says **annual applications are required**, and the state form says the same in capital letters. **This is a deferral, not forgiveness.** The county says that when the owner dies, transfers the property or stops living there, **the last three years of deferred taxes become due**, with exceptions for a spouse or co-owner who keeps the home. After a death, the county says those taxes become delinquent on the **first day of the ninth month** after the date of death. Understand that before your parent signs, especially if the plan is to sell the house or leave it to family.
- **Disabled Veteran Exclusion.** **Excludes the first $45,000** of the appraised value for an honorably discharged veteran with a **permanent and total service-connected disability**, or an unmarried surviving spouse. The county lists **no age or income restrictions**, asks for a **VA award letter**, and calls it a **one-time application**. File the AV-9 with **Form NCDVA-9**. Wayne County **Veterans Services** (below) can help with the paperwork.

**Deadline.** The county says applications are due by **June 1**. Mark **June 1** on the calendar every year for the Circuit Breaker, and once for the other two.

**Wayne revalued in 2025.** The county says it **conducted a property revaluation in 2025** and mailed new value notices in **March 2025**. Its FAQ explains why it came early: state rules require a new revaluation **within three years** when values drift below 85 percent or above 115 percent of market. The 2026 state law that pushed new values back a year applies to counties whose reappraisal took effect **January 1, 2026**, so it **does not reach Wayne**. The county has not posted the year of its next revaluation. Remember that the Elderly or Disabled Exclusion takes off half the appraised value, so the excluded part grows with a higher value.

**Rates and bills.** The county's **2026** rate is **62.59 cents per $100**. Towns add their own: **Goldsboro 69 cents** (a combined **$1.3159**), **Mount Olive 72 cents**, **Fremont 59 cents**, **Seven Springs 59 cents**, **Pikeville 54 cents**, **Eureka 53 cents** and **Walnut Creek 40 cents**. Fire districts add **6.5 to 9 cents**. Pay online through the county's **Pay Taxes Online** link, by mail or in person. We are not printing bill and interest dates because the county does not post them; call **Tax Collections at 919-731-1478** for those, and ask whether a payment plan fits a parent on a fixed income.

**Paperwork that protects the house.** The **Wayne County Register of Deeds**, **Tina Arnder**, is at **224-226 E. Walnut Street, Suite 100, Goldsboro**, **919-731-1449**, open 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. (real estate filings until 4:45 p.m.). A power of attorney that will be used for real estate is recorded there.

## Energy and utility help

**Where to apply: Wayne County DSS, 919-580-4034.** North Carolina's heating programs are applied for through **ePASS** or in person at DSS. The county's energy page lists **1560 Clingman Street** and the email **energy.assistance@waynegov.com**.

**LIEAP.** A **once-a-year payment** toward heating bills, sent straight to the vendor. Both the state and the county say households with **someone 60 or older**, or a disabled person receiving services through the NC Division of Aging and Adult Services, can sign up **December 1 to 31**; everyone else applies **January 1 to March 31** or until the money runs out. December is four weeks away; get the paperwork together now.

**CIP.** The **Crisis Intervention Program** is for heating or cooling emergencies, taken **July 1 to June 30** or until the money runs out. Wayne DSS says the most the state allows in a fiscal year is **$600**, and you need a **disconnect notice, final notice or past-due bill**. The county adds that CIP **cannot pay a bill in a deceased person's name**, which matters when a parent has died and the account was never changed. The state says a household with a member **60 or older** or disabled can have **resources at or below $4,500**.

**Too hot, not just too cold.** North Carolina's **Operation Fan Heat Relief** lists two Wayne providers: **Services on Aging (Paula Edwards, 919-705-1785)** and **WAGES (919-734-1178)**. The season has ended for this year; note it for next spring.

**The electric bill.** Much of rural Wayne is served by **Tri-County Electric Membership Corporation**, **4255 US 117 Alt, Dudley**, **919-735-2611** (24 hours), toll free **800-548-4869**. Its **Operation Round Up** fund takes applications from **individuals** for help, and it offers **budget billing**. Other homes are on **Duke Energy Progress**; Duke's **Payment Assistance Finder** on its website lists the agencies that help with its bills by ZIP code. The **Town of Pikeville** runs its own electric system and says it **does not offer individual energy assistance**; it sends residents to Wayne DSS. The **City of Goldsboro** sells water, not power; its utility customer service line is **919-580-4354**, at City Hall, 200 N. Center Street.

## Medicare, Medicaid, prescriptions

**Free Medicare counseling, in Goldsboro.** North Carolina's **Seniors' Health Insurance Information Program (SHIIP)**, run by the NC Department of Insurance, gives free, unbiased Medicare counseling at **1-855-408-1212**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Wayne County's SHIIP volunteers work through **Services on Aging**; the county says to call **919-731-1591 for an appointment**. Medicare open enrollment runs **October 15 to December 7**, so call this week. Bring the **Annual Notice of Change** that arrived in October.

**Help paying Medicare costs and prescriptions.** The **Medicare Savings Programs** can pay Medicare premiums, and **Extra Help** lowers Part D drug costs. SHIIP counselors cover both, and Social Security (**1-800-772-1213**) handles Extra Help. **NC MedAssist (1-866-331-1348)** gives free prescription medicine to **uninsured** North Carolinians with household income **below 300 percent of poverty**, and says it may help some Medicare patients case by case. We are not printing income limits for the Medicare programs; ask SHIIP to check them against your parent's numbers.

**Medicaid, and a reason to apply before December 31.** Apply online through ePASS or through Wayne DSS at **919-580-4034**; report Medicaid changes at **919-731-1022**. The **NC Medicaid Contact Center** is **888-245-0179**, and the **NC Medicaid Ombudsman** is **1-877-201-3750**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. NC Medicaid says that **if you apply by December 31, 2026, Medicaid will cover approved costs for up to 3 months before you applied**. Starting January 1, 2027, that window shrinks to **up to 2 months for adults 65 and older** and people with a disability. If a parent is heading toward a nursing home or already paying for care, that difference can be real money.

**Staying home on Medicaid: CAP/DA.** The **Community Alternatives Program for Disabled Adults (CAP/DA)** is North Carolina's Medicaid waiver that brings in-home aide help, respite and case management so someone who would otherwise qualify for a nursing home can stay home. NC Medicaid's list of case management agencies for Wayne County names **Lenoir Memorial Hospital, 252-522-7831**; **Primary Health Choice, 910-865-3500**; **RHA Management Services, 888-207-7828**; and **Walkers Home Care, 252-432-6308**. Ask DSS (**919-580-4034**) which one is taking referrals.

**Special Assistance In-Home.** Wayne DSS says **Special Assistance In-Home** gives **cash payments** to help eligible low-income adults keep living at home instead of moving to an adult care home, after an assessment by a social worker. Call **919-580-4034**.

**Mental health.** Wayne County's public mental health plan is **Trillium Health Resources**. Its **Behavioral Health Crisis Line is 888-302-0738**; member and recipient services is **877-685-2415**. (Older county guides name Eastpointe; that plan no longer serves Wayne.)

## Home repair and safety

- **In-home aides.** Services on Aging runs in-home aide help at Levels I to III (**919-731-1591**), and the county's guide lists a DSS **In-Home Aide Services** program for people **over 60 who do not receive Medicaid**, as funding allows (**919-580-4034**). Both have waits.
- **Weatherization and heating repair.** **WAGES** is the weatherization provider for **Wayne, Greene, Wilson, Pitt and Lenoir** counties, **601 Royall Avenue, Goldsboro**, **919-734-1178, extension 223**. It takes applications **year round** for low-income households, gives **special consideration to the elderly and people with disabilities**, and does the work **at no cost** to homeowners who qualify. Renters can apply with a signed statement from the owner. Its **Heating and Air Repair and Replacement Program** repairs or replaces heating and cooling equipment for eligible **elderly and disabled** people. WAGES says weatherization **cannot do major repairs** such as new roofs, floors, plumbing or electrical work. Bring proof of income, proof of ownership, and electric and gas bills.
- **Roof repair.** **Habitat for Humanity of Goldsboro-Wayne** is taking **Critical Home Repair** applications for its **"A Roof Above"** program: you must own your home in **Wayne or Lenoir County**, need a roof repair or replacement, be under its income limits (for example **$42,800 for one person, $48,900 for two**), and be **willing to pay at least a quarter of the cost**. Apply online, at **2719 Graves Drive, Suite 3, Goldsboro**, or at **919-736-9592, extension 1**.
- **Accessibility changes.** The **Independent Living Rehabilitation Program's Greenville office**, **252-999-7350**, is the state housing agency's **Displacement Prevention Partnership** partner for Wayne County; that program pays for accessibility changes that keep a person with a disability from having to move.
- **What is not open right now.** We found **no Urgent Repair or Single-Family Rehab partner for Wayne County** on the state housing agency's current partner list, and the **City of Goldsboro's CDBG home rehab program** says it is **not accepting applications**. Ask Services on Aging (**919-731-1591**) about small home repairs, or dial 2-1-1, for ramps and emergency fixes.

**Safety nets worth setting up.**
- **Friendly Call.** Free check-in calls through Services on Aging, **919-731-1591**, for a parent who lives alone.
- **CodeRED.** Wayne County Emergency Management sends weather and emergency alerts through **CodeRED**; sign your parent's phone up on the county's preparedness page.

## Transportation

**GWTA (Goldsboro-Wayne Transportation Authority).** **919-736-1374**, Monday to Friday **8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.**; the transfer center is at **103 N. Carolina Street, Goldsboro**.
- **City buses.** Five routes in Goldsboro plus a **Mount Olive connector and circulator**, **Monday to Friday 5:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.** and **Saturdays and holidays 9:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.** No Sunday service. **Seniors 60 and older and people with disabilities pay the reduced fare, 50 cents a trip** (full fare is $1). Show a birthdate document or Medicare card with a picture ID.
- **Vans anywhere in the county.** GWTA vans go anywhere in Wayne County for **$5 per person per destination**. Book **at least two business days ahead**; voicemail requests are accepted. Inside Goldsboro, be ready an hour before your appointment; elsewhere in the county, two hours. Pickups run from **5 a.m. to 6 p.m.**

- Ask GWTA whether there is a discount or grant for seniors who use the vans; we could not confirm one.

**Medicaid rides.** Wayne Medicaid members call the DSS **Transportation Line at 919-731-1095** at least **three business days** before the appointment, **by 10 a.m.** If your parent is in a Medicaid health plan, the plan's own ride line also works.

## PACE

**No PACE program serves Wayne County.**

PACE, the **Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly**, takes over a person's whole care plan so someone who needs a nursing home level of care can keep living at home. NC Medicaid's official list of the state's **11 PACE organizations** **does not name Wayne County or any Wayne ZIP code**, and there is no PACE center in Goldsboro.

- The nearest program, **Carolina SeniorCare's New Bern center**, covers **Lenoir County** next door, but **not Wayne**.
- Some older websites, including an earlier page of ours, say **LIFE St. Joseph of the Pines** in Fayetteville serves Wayne. The state's current list shows it serves **Cumberland County and portions of Harnett, Robeson, Moore and Hoke** only.

**What to use instead.** Ask Wayne DSS (**919-580-4034**) about **CAP/DA** and **Special Assistance In-Home**, ask Services on Aging (**919-731-1591**) about in-home aides and group respite, and ask the Area Agency on Aging (**252-638-3185**) what else can keep your parent at home. If a parent moves to a county a PACE program serves, the Eldercare Locator (**1-800-677-1116**) can point you to it.

## Frequently asked questions
**Mom is 74, lives in Goldsboro, and gets about $2,900 a month from Social Security and a small pension. Does she qualify for a tax break?** Likely. About $2,900 a month is about $34,800 a year, under the **$38,800** limit for 2026 and the **$39,900** limit for 2027, as long as that is the whole household's income, including a spouse's. The **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion** takes the greater of **$25,000 or half** her home's value off. File Form AV-9 by **June 1**. Call the Tax Department at **919-731-1461**.

**Should my father use the Circuit Breaker instead?** Go in with open eyes. It caps his tax at 4 or 5 percent of income, but the rest is **deferred, not forgiven**: on a sale, a death or a move, **the last three years of deferred taxes come due**. He needs **five years** of ownership and must **file every year**. If his income is under the exclusion limit, the exclusion is usually simpler. Ask the Tax Department to run both, and talk to the **Senior Legal Helpline (1-877-579-7562)** if the house is part of an estate plan.

**Mom's savings are running out and she may need Medicaid for care. Does timing matter?** It can. NC Medicaid says applications filed **by December 31, 2026** can get approved costs covered for **up to 3 months before** the application; from January 1, 2027 that drops to **2 months** for adults 65 and older. Call Wayne DSS at **919-580-4034**, and talk to the Senior Legal Helpline before moving any assets.

**Dad has dementia and Mom is wearing out. Where does she get a break?** Call Services on Aging at **919-731-1591** about **group respite** on Mondays, Wednesdays and Thursdays and the **Family Caregiver Support Program**. Call **Project C.A.R.E. at 252-414-3230** for dementia-specific help. There is no PACE program or certified adult day center in Wayne County, so ask DSS (**919-580-4034**) about **CAP/DA** if he qualifies for Medicaid.

**Mom lives alone in Mount Olive and I live out of state. What can I set up today?** Sign her up for **Friendly Call** check-ins and ask about in-home aides at Services on Aging (**919-731-1591**). Call **WAGES (919-734-1178)** about Meals on Wheels or lunch at the **Mount Olive nutrition site**. Set her up with **GWTA (919-736-1374)** for the Mount Olive connector at the 50-cent senior fare. Book a SHIIP Medicare review through Services on Aging before December 7, and mark **December 1** for her LIEAP heating application at DSS.
