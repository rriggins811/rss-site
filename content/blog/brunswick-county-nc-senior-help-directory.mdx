## Property tax relief (apply with the Brunswick County Tax Office, 910-253-2829)

**The office.** Senior and disability relief is handled by the **Brunswick County Tax Office**, **30 Government Center Drive NE, Sandifer Building, 1st Floor, Bolivia, NC 28422**. Mail goes to **PO Box 269, Bolivia, NC 28422**. The main line is **910-253-2829**, with an alternate toll-free line at **800-527-9001**. Hours are **Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.** The county's relief page asks you to call **910-253-2811**, but every other Tax Office page and the state's assessor list give **910-253-2829**, so if one number does not answer, try the other. The county publishes an online "Email the Tax Office" form but no email address. There are no satellite tax offices.

**Where to find the income limit.** Brunswick County's relief page no longer prints a dollar figure; it sends you to the state application, and the county does not have a form of its own. Older copies of that county page that still show up in search results carry a **$33,800** limit for 2023. Ignore them. The current numbers come from the North Carolina Department of Revenue's form AV-9: **$38,800 for the 2026 tax year** and **$39,900 for the 2027 tax year**.

**The three programs.** All three are set by the state and filed with the county Tax Office.

- **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion.** Takes the **greater of the first $25,000 or 50 percent** off the appraised value of the permanent residence. The owner must be **65 or older, or totally and permanently disabled**, as of **January 1**, and income must be within the limit: **$38,800 for 2026, $39,900 for 2027**. If both spouses are under 65 and one is totally and permanently disabled, you also file **Form AV-9A**, a certification of disability.
- **Circuit Breaker Tax Deferment.** Limits the tax to a percentage of the owner's income, for incomes up to **$58,200 for 2026** or **$59,850 for 2027**. It needs **five full years of ownership and five years of occupancy** before January 1. **This is a deferral, not forgiveness.** In the county's words, the taxes over the limit **"are deferred and remain a lien on the property,"** and **"the three most current years of deferred taxes prior to a disqualifying event will become due and payable, with interest."** Disqualifying events are **the death of the owner, transfer of the property, and the home no longer being the owner's permanent residence.** And the state form says it in capital letters: **you must file a new application for this program every year.** Understand the lien before your parent signs, especially if the plan is to sell the house or leave it to family.
- **Disabled Veteran Exclusion.** Takes up to the first **$45,000** off the appraised value, with **no age or income limit**. The county routes it through **Brunswick County Veterans Services, 10 Referendum Drive, Building F, Bolivia, NC 28422, 910-253-2233**, Monday to Friday 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., by appointment. Bring VA documentation dated within the past year.

**Each owner can use only one of the three programs.**

**Deadlines.** The state form says an application **must be filed by June 1** to be on time. A late application needs a showing of **good cause** and applies only to that calendar year's taxes. Income proof follows the instructions on the AV-9.

**The 2027 revaluation.** Brunswick County reappraises every four years. The last revaluation took effect **January 1, 2023**, and the next one takes effect **January 1, 2027**. The county says owners should get a notice with their **updated 2027 value by the end of March 2027**. The county rate is **34.20 cents per $100** of value (the county's own page labels that rate as the fiscal year that ended June 30, 2026, and its table shows the same 0.342 for 2026). Towns add their own rates on top. If a parent may qualify for the exclusion, the window for the 2027 tax year, **January 1 to June 1, 2027**, runs right alongside the new values.

**Paying this year's bill.** 2026 bills were mailed in mid-August and were due September 1. They can be paid **without interest through January 5, 2027**. On January 6 unpaid bills become delinquent and **2 percent interest** is added, then **0.75 percent** more on February 1 and each month after. For a payment arrangement, call the **Collections Division at 910-253-2729**. One quirk: the county collects property taxes for every town **except Holden Beach and Ocean Isle Beach**, which bill their own town taxes.

**Two protections worth ten minutes.**
- The **Register of Deeds** runs a free **Fraud Alert** service that notifies you when a document is recorded in your name, a useful guard on a parent's paid-off house. Sign up on the county website or call **910-253-2690**.
- The county also warns: **don't purchase copies of your deed from an unsolicited mailing.** Copies come from the Register of Deeds itself, 910-253-2690.

## Food

**Meals on Wheels.** **Brunswick Senior Resources** coordinates Meals on Wheels for the county. Eligible recipients get **a hot midday meal delivered on weekdays**. You must be **60 or older and homebound** (a spouse of an eligible person, or a disabled adult living with one, may also qualify), and enrollment starts with **an in-home assessment** by BSRI's Aging Resources staff. Call **910-754-2300, press 1**. BSRI does not publish waitlist status, so ask when you call.

**Lunch at the senior centers.** Registered participants **60 and older eat lunch at no charge**, with contributions of any amount encouraged; anyone under 60 pays **$5**. Reservations are advised. Meals are served at the six BSRI centers above, plus two town-run sites that BSRI sponsors:
- **Oak Island**, 102 SE 47th Street, Monday to Thursday 9:30 a.m. to 2 p.m., **910-278-3622** or **910-278-6552**
- **Boiling Spring Lakes Community Center**, 1 Leeds Road, Monday, Wednesday and Friday 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., **910-363-0018**

**Brunswick Family Assistance.** This local nonprofit runs a food pantry and emergency assistance at **929 Old Ocean Highway, Bolivia, 910-754-4766**. Hours are Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Wednesday 1 to 4:30 p.m., and Friday 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed for lunch from noon to 1 p.m.; the pantry closes 30 minutes before the office. You can use the pantry **12 times a year with at least 30 days between visits**, and you must have lived in Brunswick County **at least 90 days**. Its **mobile pantry** is at **Leland Community Park** on Mondays, Tuesdays and Fridays from 9 a.m. to noon, and makes monthly stops in Southport, Calabash, Supply, Ash and Shallotte. Some older listings show a Leland or Shallotte office; its own site now lists only Bolivia.

**The regional food bank.** The **Food Bank of Central and Eastern North Carolina's Wilmington branch**, **1000 Greenfield Street, Wilmington, 910-251-1465**, supplies pantries across Brunswick, Columbus, Duplin, New Hanover and Pender counties. Find the pantry nearest a parent's ZIP code on its "find food" page.

**Food and Nutrition Services (SNAP).** Apply online through **ePASS** at epass.nc.gov, or in person at Social Services in Bolivia with no appointment needed. Call **910-253-2077** to have an application mailed.

## Energy and utility help

**Low Income Energy Assistance (LIEAP).** A once-a-year heating payment. Brunswick County says only households with **a person 60 or older**, or a disabled person receiving services through the Division of Aging and Adult Services, can get benefits **December 1 through December 31** or until funds run out; any household can apply **January 1 through March 31**. The state's income limit is **130 percent of the federal poverty level**. Apply on **ePASS** or through Social Services, **910-253-2077**. For a parent 60 or older, **December** is the month to apply.

**Crisis Intervention Program (CIP).** For a **heating or cooling emergency**, taken **July 1 through June 30** or until the county's money runs out, so it is open now. The state limit is **150 percent of the poverty level**, with a **$4,500** resource limit for households with someone 60 or older or disabled. Apply on **ePASS** or in person at Social Services.

**If the power bill says Brunswick Electric (BEMC).** BEMC's **Warm Homes, Warm Hearts** fund helps members with their electric bill **to avoid disconnection**, and the co-op puts up **to $50,000 in matching funds**. In Brunswick County, **Brunswick Family Assistance decides eligibility and hands out the money**: call **910-754-4766** and bring the final shut-off notice. BEMC's headquarters is **795 Ocean Highway West, Supply, 910-754-4391**; member services is **800-842-5871**. BEMC does not offer budget billing to renters.

**If the power bill says Duke Energy.** Duke Energy's **Share the Light Fund** helps customers with a **past-due balance, unpaid deposit or unpaid reconnection charge**. Apply through the **NCDHHS CARELINE at 800-662-7030, press 3**, through Social Services, or at **epass.nc.gov**.

**Help with any heating fuel.** Piedmont Natural Gas's **Share the Warmth** fund, handed out through county social services outside Mecklenburg, can pay energy bills **regardless of the energy source used in the home**, and state policy says **a household does not have to be a Piedmont customer**. Income must be **at or below 150 percent** of poverty, and the benefit is capped at **$200 per state fiscal year**. Ask Social Services at **910-253-2077**.

**Brunswick Family Assistance, utilities.** Beyond the co-op fund, BFA can help eligible clients with a utility bill **once every 6 months**. Bring the final shut-off notice. It does **not** pay past-due gas or propane bills, and help depends on the money available the day you apply.

## Medicare, Medicaid, prescriptions

**Free Medicare counseling.** North Carolina's **Seniors' Health Insurance Information Program (SHIIP)**, run by the NC Department of Insurance, gives free, unbiased Medicare counseling, **by appointment**. The state's counselor locator lists three Brunswick sites, all at **910-408-1385** (ask for SHIIP help): **The Brunswick Center at Southport**, 1513 North Howe Street; **Brunswick Senior Resources**, 3620 Express Drive, Shallotte; and the **Leland SHIIP Office**, 121 Town Hall Drive. BSRI's own guide says it has SHIIP counselors at all six of its centers and books them at **910-754-2300, option 1**. The statewide SHIIP line is **1-855-408-1212**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Call before any Medicare Advantage or Part D decision, especially during open enrollment from **October 15 to December 7**.

**Help paying Medicare costs.** The **Medicare Savings Programs** can pay Medicare premiums, and **Extra Help** lowers Part D drug costs. Apply for the Medicare Savings Programs through Social Services at **910-253-2077**, and for Extra Help through SHIIP or Social Security at **1-800-772-1213**. We are not printing income limits here; ask SHIIP to check them against your parent's numbers.

**Medicaid, and a reason to apply before December 31.** Apply on ePASS, in person at Social Services in Bolivia, or by calling **910-253-2077** for a mailed application. The **NC Medicaid Contact Center** is **888-245-0179**. NC Medicaid says that for **adults 65 and older and people with a disability**, if you **apply by December 31, 2026**, Medicaid will cover approved costs for **up to 3 months before you applied**. Starting **January 1, 2027**, that look-back shrinks to **2 months**. If a parent is heading toward a nursing home or already paying for care, that difference can be real money. Work requirements starting January 1, 2027 apply to adults **19 to 64**, not to people 65 and older, and renewal moves to every six months.

**Staying home on Medicaid: CAP/DA.** The **Community Alternatives Program for Disabled Adults (CAP/DA)** is North Carolina's Medicaid waiver that brings in-home aide help, respite and case management so someone who would otherwise qualify for a nursing home can stay home. NC Medicaid's list of case management agencies for Brunswick County (dated May 2025) names **Novant Health, NHRMC, 910-667-8303**; **Primary Health Choice, 910-865-3500**; **RHA Management Services, Inc., 888-207-7828**; and **Walkers Home Care CAPDA, 252-432-6308**. Brunswick County's Adult Services page lists **910-667-8303 or 910-742-9440** for CAP/DA. The state list is more than a year old, so if a line does not answer, ask the Medicaid Contact Center.

**Special Assistance In-Home.** Brunswick County Social Services lists **Special Assistance In-Home (SA-IH)** and adult placement at **910-253-2197**. Ask whether a parent's income qualifies.

**Prescriptions.** Start with Extra Help and a SHIIP plan comparison. **NC MedAssist (1-866-331-1348)** is a free mail-order pharmacy for uninsured North Carolinians with income up to 300 percent of poverty; it says it may help **some Medicare patients on a case-by-case basis**.

## Home repair and safety

- **WARM NC.** Does **free home repairs** for owners in Bladen, **Brunswick**, Columbus, Duplin, New Hanover, Onslow and Pender counties. You must **own the home and have lived in it at least three years**, with income **at or below 50 percent** of the county median (some funders allow up to 80 percent). Its funding includes the state's **Urgent Repair Program**. **910-399-7563.** Brunswick Senior Resources says its own crisis fund pays for emergency home repairs done alongside WARM NC, so mention BSRI when you call.
- **Weatherization.** North Carolina's weatherization provider for Brunswick County is **Action Pathways**, based in Fayetteville, **910-223-0116** for weatherization (main line 910-485-6131). It serves households **at or below 200 percent** of poverty, gives **priority to the elderly and people with disabilities**, and runs the **Heating and Air Repair and Replacement Program (HARRP)** for failing furnaces and air systems. Have ID, proof of income (no bank statements), a 12-month utility printout and proof of ownership ready.
- **USDA Section 504.** For very-low-income homeowners in eligible rural areas: loans up to **$40,000** at a fixed **1 percent** over 20 years, and grants up to **$10,000** for owners **62 or older**, combinable up to $50,000. Grants must be repaid if the home is sold within three years. Check the address on USDA's eligibility map first.
- **Brunswick County Habitat for Humanity** (1323 Stone Chimney Road SW, Supply, 910-454-0007) builds homes and runs three ReStores. Its own website does not list a home repair program, so do not count on one.
- **ReBuild NC (Hurricanes Florence and Matthew): closed.** The state's homeowner recovery program says its application period is closed.

**A free daily check-in call.** The **Brunswick County Sheriff's Office "Are You OK?"** program places a **free computer-generated call every day** between **7 and 11 a.m.**, 365 days a year. If no one answers, the office tries the subscriber and the key holders, and **a deputy is sent** if no one is reached. You need **at least two key holders**. Call **910-253-2777** for an application, or mail it to PO Box 9, Bolivia, NC 28422.

**Project Lifesaver.** The Sheriff's Office also offers **free** radio-tracking bracelets for people with dementia, autism or a similar condition who are **known to wander**. Call **910-253-2777** and ask for the I.M.P.A.C.T. Unit.

## Rent, housing, emergency cash

- **Brunswick County Public Housing Agency**, **60 Government Center Drive, Building B, Bolivia, 910-253-2222.** It runs only the **Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher** program; there is no public housing. The county says the waitlist **is usually closed** and opens to the public **by lottery**. The last opening it announced was **March 11 to 25, 2024**, when it selected 250 entries. Ask to be told when it reopens.
- **Brunswick Family Assistance**, **910-754-4766.** Rent help with a late notice from the landlord, **once every 12 months**; utility help **once every 6 months**; and life-sustaining prescriptions. Help depends on eligibility **and the money available the day you apply**, and you must have lived in the county at least 90 days.
- **BSRI senior housing.** Brunswick Senior Resources keeps an **interest list for partner affordable senior apartments**, generally for incomes **below 60 percent** of the area median. Call **910-754-9846, extension 1019**.
- **2-1-1** knows which church funds and ministries have money this week.

## Transportation

**Brunswick Transit System (BTS)** is the county's public transportation, **5040 Main Street, Shallotte**. Call **910-253-7800** or toll free **877-754-2764**; TDD/TTY **800-735-2962** or 711.
- **Any Brunswick County resident can ride**, for medical trips, general purpose trips and most non-emergency destinations.
- Service runs **Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., as space permits.**
- **Book by phone at least 48 hours ahead.** Allow **2 hours** before your drop-off time, and be ready: drivers wait only **5 minutes**. Cancel at least 2 hours ahead, because no-show charges must be paid before you can book again.
- The general public fare is **$3.00 one way**, paid when you board. BTS does not list a senior discount.
- **Drivers do not enter homes**, so a parent who needs help getting out the door needs someone with them.

**BSRI rides to the senior centers.** Brunswick Senior Resources gives residents **60 and older** in select service areas free rides to its **nutrition sites and senior centers**. It is not for medical trips; for those, it points you to BTS. Call **910-754-2300**.

**Leland and Belville: Wave RideMICRO.** Wilmington's Wave Transit runs an on-demand van, **RideMICRO Zone 1, Northern Brunswick**, connecting to Wave's buses at Padgett Station in downtown Wilmington. It runs weekdays 6 a.m. to 8 p.m., Saturdays 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Sundays 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., costs **$2 per one-way trip** with no reduced fare, and is booked at **910-861-0661**. Wave does not list the towns inside the zone, so give them the address when you call. Wave's DART paratransit covers only addresses within three quarters of a mile of a Wave bus route, so ask before counting on it for a Brunswick address.

**Medicaid rides to appointments.** To apply for Medicaid transportation, Brunswick County lists **910-253-2124 or 910-253-2118**. To schedule, call **910-253-2247**. The county asks for **3 business days' notice** for trips inside the county and **at least 5 business days** for trips outside it, such as to a Wilmington specialist. (NC Medicaid's statewide rule is at least 2 days for health plan members, who call their plan, and 4 days for NC Medicaid Direct members. The county's own rule is longer, so go by the county.)

## Legal help

**Senior Legal Helpline.** **Legal Aid of North Carolina's Senior Legal Helpline** gives **free legal help to North Carolinians 60 and older**: **1-877-579-7562**, Monday to Friday **9 to 11 a.m. and 1 to 3 p.m.** Its Senior Law Project covers public benefits like Medicaid, food stamps and SSI, with priority for those with the greatest economic need.

**Legal Aid, Wilmington office.** **272 North Front Street, Suite 220, Wilmington, 910-763-6207**, Monday to Friday 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., serves **Bladen, Brunswick, Columbus, Duplin, New Hanover, Onslow and Pender** counties. To apply for help, Legal Aid points people to its statewide helpline, **1-866-219-5262**, Monday to Friday 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.

**Protecting an older adult.**
- **Brunswick County Adult Protective Services:** **910-253-2077** or **910-253-2415** during business hours. **After hours, call 911** and ask for the on-call social worker. Do not email a report.
- **Long-Term Care Ombudsman at the Cape Fear Council of Governments**, for problems in a nursing home or assisted living: **910-444-8485** or **910-444-8198**.
- **Assisted living complaints** can also go to the county's Adult Home Specialist at **910-253-2166**, or the state Division of Health Service Regulation at **800-624-3004**.
- **Guardianship questions**, Brunswick County Social Services: **910-253-2123**.
- **NC Attorney General Consumer Protection: 1-877-566-7226** (1-877-5-NO-SCAM).
- **National Elder Fraud Hotline: 1-833-372-8311.**

**Paperwork offices you will need.** A power of attorney that will be used for real estate is recorded at the **Brunswick County Register of Deeds, 75 Courthouse Drive NE, Building I, Bolivia, 910-253-2690**. Estates run through the **Brunswick County Clerk of Superior Court, 310 Government Center Drive NE, Bolivia**, main line **910-253-3900**, estates **910-253-3904**. For a veteran's benefits, **Brunswick County Veterans Services, 910-253-2233**.

## Caregiver support

- **BSRI Family Caregiver Support.** Support groups, **respite care**, and supplemental services on a limited basis, including the **Homecare Independence Program**, which lets a caregiver **hire family, friends or others** to help. ## PACE

**Brunswick County has a PACE program, but only in the northern and eastern ZIP codes.**

PACE, the **Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly**, takes over a person's whole care plan, doctors, a day center, therapy, personal care, prescriptions and transportation, so someone who needs a nursing home level of care can keep living at home. NC Medicaid's official service area list names **Elderhaus, 2222 South 17th Street, Wilmington, NC 28401**, serving **New Hanover and Brunswick** counties. The phone the state list gives is **910-767-0932**.

**Who qualifies.** People **55 and older** who meet a **nursing home level of care** and can live safely in the community with PACE's support, inside the service area.

**Check the ZIP code.** The state list names only these Brunswick ZIP codes: **28422 (Bolivia), 28451 (Leland), 28461 (Southport) and 28479 (Winnabow)**. It does **not** list Oak Island (28465), Shallotte (28470), Supply (28462), Calabash (28467), Ocean Isle Beach (28469), Sunset Beach (28468), Ash (28420) or Longwood (28452). If your parent lives in one of those, call Elderhaus before you build a plan around it, and ask about CAP/DA through the agencies above instead.

## Frequently asked questions
**Mom is 72, lives in Leland, and gets about $3,000 a month from Social Security and a small pension. Does she qualify for a tax break?** Likely. About $3,000 a month is about $36,000 a year, under the **$38,800** limit for 2026 and the **$39,900** limit for 2027. The **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion** takes the greater of **$25,000 or half** of her home's value off the tax bill. Her window for the 2027 tax year is **January 1 to June 1, 2027**, the same spring the county mails new 2027 values. Call the Tax Office at **910-253-2829** in January.

**Dad's new value comes in March 2027. Should he use the Circuit Breaker?** Go in with open eyes. It limits his tax to a percentage of income, but the rest is **deferred, not forgiven**. It stays a lien, and when he dies, the home is transferred, or it stops being his permanent residence, **the last three years of deferred taxes come due with interest**, usually out of the sale. He must **reapply every year**, and he needs five years of ownership and occupancy first. If his income is under the exclusion limit, the exclusion is usually simpler, and each owner can use only one program. Ask the Tax Office to run both, and talk to the **Senior Legal Helpline (1-877-579-7562)** if the house is part of an estate plan.

**Mom lives in Oak Island and needs a nursing home level of care. Can PACE keep her home?** Not by the state's list. Elderhaus's Brunswick service area covers only Bolivia, Leland, Southport and Winnabow ZIP codes, and Oak Island's **28465** is not one of them. Call Elderhaus at **910-767-0932** to confirm, then look at **CAP/DA** through the case management agencies above, and ask Social Services about **Special Assistance In-Home** at **910-253-2197**.

**Dad's savings are running out and he may need Medicaid for care. Does timing matter?** It can. NC Medicaid says people 65 and older who **apply by December 31, 2026** can get approved costs covered for **up to 3 months before they applied**; from January 1, 2027, the look-back is **2 months**. Call Social Services at **910-253-2077** or apply on ePASS, and talk to the Senior Legal Helpline before moving any assets.

**My mother in Shallotte has a specialist in Wilmington and no car. How does she get there?** If she has Medicaid, call **910-253-2247** at least **5 business days** ahead; that is the county's rule for out-of-county trips. If she does not, **Brunswick Transit System (910-253-7800)** takes any county resident for medical trips with 48 hours' notice at **$3 one way**; ask when you book whether the Wilmington trip is on its schedule. Drivers do not come inside, so plan for someone to help her to the door.

**Mom lives alone near Southport and hurricane season scares me. What can I set up today?** Three things. Enroll her in the Sheriff's free **"Are You OK?"** daily call (**910-253-2777**), which sends a deputy if she does not answer and no key holder can be reached. Sign her house up for the **Register of Deeds Fraud Alert**. And call **Brunswick Senior Resources (910-754-2300, press 1)** so she is known to the county's aging network before a storm, not after.
