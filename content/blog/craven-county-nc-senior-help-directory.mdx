## Property tax relief (apply with the Craven County Tax Department, 252-636-6604)

**The office.** Senior and disability relief is handled by the **Craven County Tax Department**, **226 Pollock Street, New Bern, NC 28560** (mail to **P.O. Box 1128, New Bern, NC 28563-1128**), **Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.** The county's relief pages say exclusions and exemptions are handled by the **Personal Property Division** at **252-636-6604** (email tax@cravencountync.gov). The **Tax Administrator is Leslie Young**, whom the state's official list of county assessors (revised September 23, 2026) names as Craven's County Assessor. Other lines: **Real Estate Appraisal 252-636-6640**, **Tax Collection 252-636-6605**.

**The three programs.** All three are set by the state and filed on the state's **Form AV-9** with the county. The county posts a separate page and application for each.

- **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion** (the county's pages call it the Elderly Homestead Exclusion and the Disabled Homestead Exclusion). It **excludes the greater of $25,000 or 50 percent of the appraised value of the home and up to one acre of land**. You must be **65 or older on January 1, or totally and permanently disabled**, be a North Carolina resident, and own and occupy the home by January 1. Income for the prior year must be within the limit: **$38,800 for the 2026 tax year, $39,900 for 2027**. The county counts "all monies received from every source" other than gifts or inheritances from a spouse, parent or child, and lists Social Security, pensions, IRA and 401(k) distributions, annuities, interest, dividends, rental income, wages and VA benefits among them. For a married couple living together, **both incomes count**, even if only one name is on the deed. The county also says a parent **does not lose the exclusion for a temporary absence for health reasons or a stay in a nursing home**, as long as the home sits empty or a spouse or dependent lives there.
- **Circuit Breaker Tax Deferment.** Caps the tax at **4 percent of income up to $38,800** and **5 percent from $38,801 to $58,200** for 2026 (for 2027 the ceilings are **$39,900** and **$59,850**). The owner must be **65 or older or totally and permanently disabled** and must have **owned and lived in the home at least five years**. The state's form says in capital letters that **you must file a new application every year**. **This is a deferral, not forgiveness.** The county's page quotes the law: the deferred taxes stay a **lien on the home**, and the deferred taxes for the **preceding three years** come due with interest when the owner sells or transfers the home, dies, or stops living there (with exceptions for a spouse or co-owner who stays). Understand that before your parent signs, especially if the plan is to sell the house or leave it to family.
- **Disabled Veteran Exclusion.** **Excludes the first $45,000** of the appraised value for an honorably discharged veteran with a **permanent and total service-connected disability**, or who received specially adapted housing benefits, or an unmarried surviving spouse. The county lists **no age or income requirement** and calls it a **one-time application**. File the AV-9 with **Form NCDVA-9**, which the county links from its page. Craven County **Veterans Services** (below) can help with the paperwork.

**Deadline.** The county says applications should be filed during the regular listing period in January but **must be accepted through June 1**. Mark **June 1** on the calendar every year for the Circuit Breaker, and once for the other two.

**If your parent sells or dies mid-year.** The county says that if the home is **sold between January 1 and July 1**, the exclusion comes off for that tax year; if the sale or death happens **after July 1**, the exclusion stays for that year.

**No 2026 revaluation in Craven.** The Tax Department says Craven's **last revaluation was completed in 2023** and it is now conducting the next countywide revaluation for **tax year 2028**; it plans to post comparable sales in **February 2028**. That means the 2026 state law that pushed new values back a year in counties that revalued in 2026 **does not apply here**. When the 2028 values arrive, remember that the Elderly or Disabled Exclusion takes off half the appraised value, so the excluded part grows with a higher value.

**Rates and bills.** The Board of Commissioners kept the county rate at **44.48 cents per $100** in the 2026-2027 budget. Towns and fire districts add their own rates; the county's **2026 rate sheet** lists every one, from a total of about **46 cents** in some fire districts to about **$1.19 in Havelock**. The same sheet lists a **$72 recycle fee**, and **$30** for elderly or disabled and disabled veteran accounts; ask the Tax Department whether your parent's bill gets the lower fee. The county says **tax notices are mailed in August**, are **due September 1**, and can be paid **without interest through January 5**; interest starts **January 6**. Pay online through **myCravenCountyNC**, which the county says supports **scheduled and partial payments**, or by mail or in person. Collections: **252-636-6605**.

**Paperwork that protects the house.** The **Craven County Register of Deeds** is in the same building, **226 Pollock Street, New Bern**, **252-636-6617**. A power of attorney that will be used for real estate is recorded there. Its page lists a **Recordation Notification** service; ask the office how to sign your parent up to hear when a document is recorded under their name.

## Food

**Home-delivered meals at 60.** Craven County Senior Services delivers a hot meal on weekdays to residents who are **60 or older**, **homebound** and **unable to prepare a full meal**. The county says it serves an average of **150 to 160 meals a day** in **New Bern, Havelock, Vanceboro, Ernul, Cove City and Dover**. Most routes get one hot meal a day; a few get **one hot and four frozen meals once a week**. In bad weather the county may send shelf-stable meals ahead. Call **252-638-1790** to ask about eligibility and whether there is a wait. The program also needs volunteer drivers, especially on Mondays and Wednesdays.

**Lunch with company.** The **George Street Senior Center** in New Bern is a congregate meal site; call **252-638-1790** for the schedule and how to sign up. In Havelock, the **Havelock Senior Center** serves **lunch Monday through Friday at noon** through the state's Congregate Meals Program for eligible seniors; the city says volunteers provide privately funded meals for others **when those funds are available**. Applications and lunch sign-up sheets are at the center's office, **252-444-6445**.

**The regional food bank.** The **Food Bank of Central & Eastern North Carolina's New Bern branch**, **1702 Red Robin Lane, New Bern**, **252-577-1912**, Monday to Friday 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., serves **Carteret, Craven, Jones, Onslow and Pamlico** counties. It supplies local pantries; for the nearest one, dial 2-1-1 and call before you go.

**Local pantries the city lists.** The City of New Bern's utility assistance page names **Religious Community Services**, **919 George Street, New Bern**, **252-633-2767**, and **Catholic Charities**, **502 Middle Street, New Bern**, **252-638-2188**. The Area Agency on Aging's resource sheet says both run food pantries. Call for hours.

**Food and Nutrition Services (SNAP).** Apply online through **ePASS** (epass.nc.gov) or call Craven County DSS at **252-636-4900**.

## Energy and utility help

**Where to apply: Craven County DSS, 252-636-4900.** North Carolina's heating programs are applied for through **ePASS** or at Craven DSS, 2818 Neuse Boulevard.

**LIEAP.** A **once-a-year payment** toward heating bills, sent straight to the vendor. Both the state and the county say households with **someone 60 or older**, or a disabled person receiving services through the NC Division of Aging and Adult Services, can sign up **December 1 to 31**; everyone else applies **January 1 to March 31** or until the money runs out.

**CIP.** The **Crisis Intervention Program** is for heating or cooling emergencies, taken **July 1 to June 30** or until the money runs out. Craven DSS says the benefit can be **up to $600 per crisis**, but **no more than $600 per state fiscal year**, paid directly to the vendor. The state says a household with a member **60 or older** or disabled can have **resources at or below $4,500**, must have a **health-related or life-threatening emergency**, and can bring a final notice or past-due bill.

**Too hot, not just too cold.** The state's 2026 **Operation Fan Heat Relief** list names two Craven providers: **Craven County Senior Services, 252-638-1790**, and the **Havelock Senior Center, 252-444-6445**. The program runs through **October 31**, so if your parent needs a fan this season, call this week; otherwise, note it for next May.

**The electric bill.** If your parent is a **City of New Bern** utility customer, the city's customer service line is **252-639-2750**, weekdays 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The city says bills are due when received, a **5 percent late penalty** is charged the day after the due date, and service can be interrupted ten days later. A customer who cannot pay by the due date can **visit the office with photo ID** to ask for a payment arrangement; the city says **time extensions are not handled by phone**, and the account holder can authorize someone else to go. The city's page also points to **DSS, Religious Community Services, Catholic Charities, Coastal Community Action and the Salvation Army (1402 Rhem Avenue, 252-637-2277, appointment required)** for help with the bill. Much of the rest of the county is served by **Carteret-Craven Electric Cooperative**, with an office in **Havelock**, main line **252-247-3107**; ask member services about payment arrangements and how to flag a home with medical equipment.

## Medicare, Medicaid, prescriptions

**Free Medicare counseling.** North Carolina's **Seniors' Health Insurance Information Program (SHIIP)**, run by the NC Department of Insurance, gives free, unbiased Medicare counseling at **1-855-408-1212**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Medicare open enrollment runs **October 15 to December 7**. Ask Senior Services (**252-638-1790**) whether a SHIIP counselor is seeing people in Craven County this fall. Call before any Medicare Advantage or Part D decision, and read the **Annual Notice of Change** that arrives in October.

**Help paying Medicare costs and prescriptions.** The **Medicare Savings Programs** can pay Medicare premiums, and **Extra Help** lowers Part D drug costs. SHIIP counselors cover both, and Social Security (**1-800-772-1213**) handles Extra Help. **NC MedAssist (1-866-331-1348)** gives free prescription medicine to **uninsured** North Carolinians with household income **below 300 percent of poverty**. We are not printing income limits for the Medicare programs; ask SHIIP to check them against your parent's numbers.

**Medicaid, and a reason to apply before December 31.** Apply online through ePASS or through Craven DSS at **252-636-4900**. The **NC Medicaid Contact Center** is **888-245-0179**, and the **NC Medicaid Ombudsman** is **1-877-201-3750**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. NC Medicaid says that **if you apply by December 31, 2026, Medicaid will cover approved costs for up to 3 months before you applied**. Starting January 1, 2027, that window shrinks to **up to 2 months for adults 65 and older** and people with a disability. If a parent is heading toward a nursing home or already paying for care, that difference can be real money.

**Staying home on Medicaid: CAP/DA.** The **Community Alternatives Program for Disabled Adults (CAP/DA)** is North Carolina's Medicaid waiver that brings in-home aide help, respite and case management so someone who would otherwise qualify for a nursing home can stay home. NC Medicaid's list of case management agencies for Craven County names **CarolinaEast Medical Center, 252-633-8182**; **Lenoir Memorial Hospital, 252-522-7831**; **RHA Management Services, 888-207-7828**; and **Walkers Home Care, 252-432-6308**. Ask DSS Adult Services (**252-636-4900**) which one is taking referrals.

**Personal care and Special Assistance In-Home.** Craven DSS says Medicaid members may qualify for **Personal Care Services** with a doctor's referral, and that low-income adults at risk of moving to an adult care home may qualify for **Special Assistance In-Home**, which helps with basic living expenses so they can stay home. Ask Adult Services Intake at **252-636-4900**.

**Mental health.** Craven County's public mental health plan is **Trillium Health Resources**. Its **Behavioral Health Crisis Line is 888-302-0738**; member and recipient services is **877-685-2415**.

## Home repair and safety

- **In-home aides.** Craven DSS provides **in-home aide services**, mainly personal care and hygiene, for adults whose functioning is impaired, to prevent decline and a premature move to a facility. Call **252-636-4900** and ask for Adult Services Intake.
- **Weatherization and heating repair.** North Carolina's weatherization provider for Craven County is **Coastal Community Action**, **303 McQueen Avenue, Newport, NC 28570** (P.O. Box 729), main line **252-223-1630**, weatherization **252-223-1645**, serving **Carteret, Craven, Duplin, Jones, New Hanover, Onslow, Pamlico and Beaufort**. The agency says households **at or below 200 percent** of poverty, or on **Work First or SSI**, qualify, and it puts **the elderly and people with disabilities** first. Owners and renters (with the landlord's written permission) can apply, for single-family and mobile homes. It also runs the state's **Heating and Air Repair and Replacement Program**. We are not printing dollar limits; ask the agency to check your parent's numbers.
- **Home rehab.** The NC Housing Finance Agency's partner list names three **Single-Family Rehab** partners for Craven: **Craven County** itself (**406 Craven Street, New Bern, 252-636-6600**), **Coastal Community Action** (**252-223-1630**), and **North Carolina Baptist Men (Baptists on Mission)** (**800-395-5102**). Separately, the county won a **$950,000 Community Development Block Grant** for substantial rehab and reconstruction of owner-occupied homes; it says **five homes and one alternate have already been selected** and construction should start in spring or summer 2027, so that round is closed.
- **Urgent Repair.** We found **no Urgent Repair partner for Craven County** on the state housing agency's current partner list. Ask the Area Agency on Aging (**252-638-3185**) or 2-1-1 who is doing emergency repairs and ramps right now.
- **Accessibility changes.** **Independent Living Rehabilitation Program**, **2832 Neuse Boulevard, New Bern**, **252-649-6720**, is the state housing agency's **Displacement Prevention Partnership** partner for Carteret, Craven, Jones, Onslow and Pamlico; that program pays for accessibility modifications that keep a person with a disability from having to move. Ask whether Craven applications are open.

**Safety nets worth setting up.**
- **Project Lifesaver (New Bern).** The New Bern Police Department and New Bern Fire-Rescue run **Project Lifesaver** with the Area Agency on Aging for people with dementia, autism or other conditions who are prone to wandering. Enrollees wear a small radio transmitter band, and the city says there should be **no cost** to enrollees who qualify, based on grant funds. The Area Agency on Aging handles intake: **January Brown, 252-638-3185, extension 3011**. Its resource sheet says the service is for **New Bern residents only, not River Bend or Trent Woods**.
- **Havelock Senior Safety Initiative.** For seniors and disabled adults who **live alone in the City of Havelock**, Havelock police dispatch makes **two check-in calls a week**, and if nobody answers, an officer does a **wellness check**. Your parent needs a working phone and must give the department a way into the home (key, door code or lockbox). Call **Captain Anthony Fischer, 252-447-3212, extension 3131**.
- **Special Needs Registry.** Craven DSS keeps a **Special Needs Registry** of residents who may need help in a hurricane or other disaster, so the county can reach them before it hits. The application is on the Adult Services page, or call **252-636-4900**.

## Transportation

**CARTS (Craven Area Rural Transit System).** **1106 Clarks Road, New Bern, NC 28562**, **252-636-4917**, TDD/TTY **800-735-2962**, office open **Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.** CARTS serves **Craven, Jones and Pamlico** counties, is **open to the public**, and says all of its vehicles are **ADA accessible**.
- **Demand response.** Rides anywhere in Craven, Jones and Pamlico counties, booked **48 business hours ahead**, from **5 a.m. to 5 p.m.** weekdays. The county says the fare **starts at $3.25 and varies by mileage**. Tell the dispatcher if your parent uses the wheelchair lift or brings an attendant. The vehicle arrives within 15 minutes of the scheduled time and may stop for other riders.
- **EDTAP.** The **Elderly and Disabled Transportation Assistance Program** is a grant that helps Craven residents **60 or older or with a permanent disability** with ride costs. Apply online or ask CARTS to mail an application.
- **Fixed routes in New Bern.** Two routes, a bus at each stop **every hour, 7 a.m. to 5 p.m.** weekdays, **$1 a ride**, including a stop at the **New Bern Mall**.
- **Same-day rides (OnDemand+).** Since October 1, 2026, CARTS takes same-day requests across much of New Bern from **10 a.m. to 2 p.m.** for **$5 a ride**, when a vehicle is nearby. Not guaranteed.
- **Greenville Express.** A pilot run to Greenville, boarding at the New Bern Mall, arriving **10 a.m.** and leaving **2 p.m.** on **Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays**. **$20 one way or $40 round trip**, paid before boarding; book at least **two days ahead**.
- **Rules to know.** Cancel **before 5 p.m. one business day before** the trip, or a fee may be charged. Drivers need **exact cash** and do not make change.

**Medicaid rides.** Medicaid members ask Craven DSS about **Medicaid transportation** at **252-636-4900**.

## Legal help

**Senior Legal Helpline.** **Legal Aid of North Carolina's Senior Legal Helpline** gives **free civil legal help to North Carolinians 60 and older**: **1-877-579-7562**, Monday to Friday **9 to 11 a.m. and 1 to 3 p.m.**

**Legal Aid of North Carolina, Greenville office.** Serves Craven and 20 other eastern counties from **301 S. Evans Street, Suite 400, Greenville, NC 27858** (mail to P.O. Box 7283, Greenville, NC 27835), **252-758-0113**, toll free **1-800-682-4592**, Monday to Friday **9 a.m. to 5 p.m.** The Area Agency on Aging also lists **legal services** for basic needs like wills and powers of attorney among the community programs it funds; ask at **252-638-3185**.

**Protecting an older adult.**
- **Craven County Adult Protective Services:** call DSS at **252-636-4900**. Reports are taken **24 hours a day, 365 days a year**; on nights, weekends and holidays, call the **Craven County Sheriff's Office at 252-633-2357** and ask for the on-call social worker. If someone is in immediate danger, **call 911**. North Carolina law requires anyone who suspects a disabled adult is being abused, neglected or exploited to report it.
- **Adult care homes.** Craven DSS monitors licensed adult care homes at least quarterly and investigates complaints; call **252-636-4900**.
- **Long-Term Care Ombudsman**, for problems in a nursing home or assisted living: the **Eastern Carolina Council Area Agency on Aging, 252-638-3185**. The state's **nursing home complaint hotline** is **1-800-624-3004**.
- **NC Attorney General Consumer Protection: 1-877-566-7226** (1-877-5-NO-SCAM).
- **National Elder Fraud Hotline: 1-833-372-8311.**

**Paperwork offices you will need.** Powers of attorney for real estate are recorded at the **Register of Deeds, 226 Pollock Street, New Bern, 252-636-6617**. Wills and estates are handled by the **Clerk of Superior Court** at the **Craven County Courthouse, 302 Broad Street, New Bern** (mail to P.O. ## PACE

**One PACE program covers all of Craven County.**

PACE, the **Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly**, takes over a person's whole care plan, doctors, a day center, therapy, personal care, prescriptions and transportation, so someone who needs a nursing home level of care can keep living at home. NC Medicaid's official PACE list shows:

- **Carolina SeniorCare, New Bern center**, **3681 Neuse Boulevard, New Bern, NC 28562**, **252-597-9008** (TTY 1-800-735-2962), for **all of Craven, Jones, Lenoir and Pamlico** counties and **portions of Beaufort, Carteret and Onslow**. The program's own page lists the New Bern, Havelock, Vanceboro, Dover, Cove City, Bridgeton and Ernul ZIP codes among those it serves. Participants must be **55 or older**, qualify for **nursing facility level of care** as determined by the state Medicaid agency, live in the service area and be able to live safely at home with PACE support. Once enrolled there are **no deductibles, copays or coinsurance**; people with Medicare only pay a set monthly fee, and Medicaid members pay a small monthly amount or nothing for the long-term care part.

No other PACE program on the state list serves Craven County. The program says eligibility is subject to verification of residency by ZIP code, so **call to confirm it serves your parent's address and has room** before you plan around it.

**If PACE does not fit.** Ask DSS Adult Services (**252-636-4900**) about **CAP/DA**, **Personal Care Services** and **Special Assistance In-Home**, and ask the Area Agency on Aging (**252-638-3185**) what else can keep your parent at home.

## Frequently asked questions
**Mom is 74, lives in Trent Woods, and gets about $2,900 a month from Social Security and a small pension. Does she qualify for a tax break?** Likely. About $2,900 a month is about $34,800 a year, under the **$38,800** limit for 2026 and the **$39,900** limit for 2027, as long as that is the whole household's income, including a spouse's. The **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion** takes the greater of **$25,000 or half** her home's value off. File Form AV-9 by **June 1**. Call the Tax Department's Personal Property Division at **252-636-6604**.

**Should my father use the Circuit Breaker instead?** Go in with open eyes. It caps his tax at 4 or 5 percent of income, but the rest is **deferred, not forgiven**: on a sale, a death or a move, **the deferred taxes for the preceding three years come due with interest**. He needs **five years** of ownership and must **file every year**. If his income is under the exclusion limit, the exclusion is usually simpler. Ask the Tax Department to run both, and talk to the **Senior Legal Helpline (1-877-579-7562)** if the house is part of an estate plan.

**Dad moved into a nursing home. Does he lose the tax break on his house?** Not by itself. The county says an owner does not lose the exclusion because of an extended stay in a rest home or nursing home, as long as the house is empty or a spouse or dependent lives there. If the house is sold, timing matters: a sale between January 1 and July 1 takes the exclusion off for that year. Call **252-636-6604** before you list it.

**Mom's savings are running out and she may need Medicaid for care. Does timing matter?** It can. NC Medicaid says applications filed **by December 31, 2026** can get approved costs covered for **up to 3 months before** the application; from January 1, 2027 that drops to **2 months** for adults 65 and older. Call Craven DSS at **252-636-4900**, and talk to the Senior Legal Helpline before moving any assets.

**Dad has dementia and has wandered once already. What can we do?** If he lives in New Bern (not River Bend or Trent Woods), ask the Area Agency on Aging about **Project Lifesaver**: **252-638-3185, extension 3011**. Sign him up for the county's **Special Needs Registry** through DSS. Take a break at the **Sunshine Center** on Wednesday afternoons and try the **Memory Cafe** on the first Monday (**252-638-1790**). If he needs a nursing home level of care, **Carolina SeniorCare (252-597-9008)** serves the whole county.

**Mom lives alone in Havelock and I live out of state. What can I set up today?** Call **Captain Anthony Fischer at 252-447-3212, extension 3131** about the **Havelock Senior Safety Initiative**, two check-in calls a week. Get her signed up for weekday lunch at the **Havelock Senior Center (252-444-6445)**, ask **Senior Services (252-638-1790)** about home-delivered meals if she is homebound, set her up with **CARTS (252-636-4917)** and an **EDTAP** application for rides, and book a SHIIP Medicare review at **1-855-408-1212** before December 7.
