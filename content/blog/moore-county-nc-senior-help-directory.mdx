## Property tax relief (apply with the Moore County Tax Department, 910-947-2255)

**The office.** Senior and disability relief is handled by the **Moore County Tax Department**, **1 Courthouse Square, Carthage, NC 28327**, **Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.**, phone **910-947-2255**, email taxinformation@moorecountync.gov. Mail goes to **P.O. Box 457, Carthage, NC 28327**. The county's 2026 exclusion brochures list **910-947-4121** for questions about the exclusions. The state's official list of county assessors (revised September 23, 2026) names **Gary Briggs** as Moore's assessor at **910-947-2255**.

**One thing to watch on the county's site.** The "Elderly or Disabled Exclusion" link on the Tax Department's Listing page opens an **older brochure that shows a $33,800 income limit**. That number is out of date. The county's **2026** brochure says **$38,800**, which matches the state's 2026 form. Use the figures below, and confirm with the office.

**The three programs.** All three are set by the state and filed on the state's **Form AV-9** with the county.

- **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion** (the county's brochure calls it the Senior or Disabled Homestead Exclusion). It **excludes the greater of $25,000 or 50 percent of the assessed value of the home and up to one acre of land**. You must be **65 or older, or totally and permanently disabled**, and own and live in the home. Income, including a spouse's, must be within the limit: **$38,800 for the 2026 tax year, $39,900 for 2027** (2026 income). The county says that if something changes, such as income going over the limit, you must **tell the Tax Department in January** of the following year. It also says a home can keep the exclusion during an **extended stay in a rest home or nursing home**, as long as the house is empty or lived in by the owner's spouse or another dependent. If your parent is under 65 and disabled, add **Form AV-9A**.
- **Circuit Breaker Tax Deferment.** Caps the tax at **4 percent of income up to $38,800** and **5 percent from $38,801 to $58,200** for 2026 (for 2027 the ceilings are **$39,900** and **$59,850**). Above the top limit, the county says, a household **does not qualify**. The owner must have **owned the home at least five years**, and the county says it **requires an annual application**. **This is a deferral, not forgiveness.** In the county's words, the current year plus the **three most recent years of deferred taxes, plus interest**, are payable when a **disqualifying event** happens, such as a sale, a death or a move. Understand that before your parent signs, especially if the plan is to sell the house or leave it to family.
- **Disabled Veteran Exclusion.** **Excludes the first $45,000** of the appraised value for an honorably discharged veteran with a **100 percent total and permanent service-connected disability**, or an unmarried surviving spouse. There is **no age or income limit**, and the county says it is a **one-time application**. File the AV-9 plus **Form NCDVA-9**, signed and certified by a Veterans Service Officer. The county warns the certification **can take two to three weeks** to come back, so start early. Moore County Veterans Services (below) can help.

**Deadline.** A timely application is due **by June 1** of the year you want relief. The county says that after June 1 **an additional form is required**, so call **910-947-4121** before you file late.

**The 2027 revaluation.** Moore County revalues **every four years**, more often than the eight years state law requires. Today's values took effect **January 1, 2023**, and the county says **the next revaluation is scheduled for 2027**; it has already posted its **2027 Schedule of Values**. Each year's appeal process **begins January 1** with an informal review, and the formal step is a review by the **Moore County Board of Equalization and Review**. The chance to appeal **ends when that Board adjourns**, so open the new value notice right away and call **910-947-2255** if the value looks wrong. The county does not post the Board's 2027 meeting dates yet.

The county's pages do not say how a revaluation affects relief that is already approved, so call when the 2027 value arrives to confirm your parent's status. If a parent's income is under the 2027 limit and they are not yet on the exclusion, **June 1, 2027** is the date to circle.

**Rates and bills.** The county's rate chart shows a **2026 county rate of 29 cents per $100** of value and an **ALS rate of 4.25 cents**. The fire protection district rate is **8.75 cents**. Towns add their own: **Aberdeen 42 cents**, **Cameron 55.5**, **Carthage 50.5**, **Foxfire 29**, **Pinebluff 40**, **Pinehurst 22**, **Robbins 68**, **Southern Pines 29** (plus a downtown service district), **Taylortown 47.5**, **Vass 49** and **Whispering Pines 40**. Carthage, Robbins and Southern Pines also list vehicle tag fees. The chart marks every town except **Pinebluff** as one the county collects for, so if your parent lives in Pinebluff, ask the town how its tax is billed.

Real property taxes are **due September 1**. The county gives a **discount to owners who pay before August 31**; the rate chart notes that **Aberdeen, Pinehurst and Southern Pines** did not offer the 2 percent discount. Bills are **past due if not paid by January 5**, and the county says penalties are added for each month after that. Pay in person at **1 Courthouse Square** by cash, check or card, by mail to P.O. Box 457, online through the county's **InvoiceCloud** page, or by phone at **1-855-451-2620**.

**One protection worth ten minutes.** The **Moore County Register of Deeds** offers a free **Real Estate Recording Notification Service**: it emails you when a document is recorded under a name you choose, **up to 10 names or name variations per email address**. Sign up from the Register of Deeds page. The office is at **26 Courthouse Square, Carthage**, **910-947-6370**.

## Food

It delivers **weekday meals to homebound residents 60 and older** in the **West End and Seven Lakes, Robbins, Carthage, Eagle Springs, Cameron, Vass, Whispering Pines and Taylortown** areas. Call **Rhonda Priest at 910-947-4185** to sign up. The county does not post a cost or a waiting list, so ask both when you call.

**If your parent lives in Southern Pines, Pinehurst or Aberdeen.** Those towns are not on the county's delivery list. **Meals on Wheels of the Sandhills**, a nonprofit at **1975 Juniper Lake Road, Unit A1, West End**, says it serves **homebound people in a portion of Moore County**. Call **910-704-9810** and ask whether your parent's street is on a route and what the meals cost.

**Ensure at a lower price.** The Department of Aging sells **Ensure for $18 a case** to people **60 and older** with a **doctor's prescription** for it and income **no higher than 150 percent of the federal poverty level**. Call **910-947-4483**.

**The regional food bank.** The **Food Bank of Central & Eastern North Carolina's Sandhills branch**, **195 Sandy Avenue, Southern Pines**, **910-692-5959**, serves **Lee, Moore, Richmond and Scotland** counties, Monday to Friday 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. It supplies local pantries; for the nearest one, dial 2-1-1 and call before you go.

**Food and Nutrition Services (SNAP).** Apply online through **ePASS** (epass.nc.gov) or call Moore County Social Services at **910-947-2436**.

## Energy and utility help

**Where to apply: Moore County Social Services, 910-947-2436.** North Carolina's heating programs are applied for through **ePASS** or Moore DSS.

**LIEAP.** A **once-a-year payment** toward heating bills, sent straight to the vendor. The state says households with **someone 60 or older**, or a disabled person getting services through the NC Division of Aging and Adult Services, can sign up **December 1 to 31**; everyone else applies **January 1 to March 31** or until the money runs out. Moore DSS posts the same windows.

**CIP.** The **Crisis Intervention Program** is for heating or cooling emergencies, taken **July 1 to June 30** or until the money runs out. The state sets income at **150 percent** of poverty, households with a member **60 or older** or disabled can have **resources at or below $4,500**, and the household must have a **health-related or life-threatening emergency**. Moore County does not post a dollar cap, so ask DSS.

**Emergency Assistance.** Moore DSS also lists an **Emergency Assistance** program for households with income **at or below 200 percent** of poverty. Ask what it covers this year.

**Too hot, not just too cold.** The state's 2026 **Operation Fan Heat Relief** list names the **Moore County Department of Aging in West End, 910-947-4483**, contact **Rhonda Priest**. The state says fans go out **May 1 to October 31, 2026**, so if your parent needs one this season, call before the end of the month.

**Water bills.** If your parent's water bill comes from the **Town of Southern Pines**, billing questions go to **910-692-2206**. We found no water bill assistance program posted by the county or the towns.

## Medicare, Medicaid, prescriptions

**Free Medicare counseling.** North Carolina's **Seniors' Health Insurance Information Program (SHIIP)**, run by the NC Department of Insurance, gives free, unbiased Medicare counseling. In Moore County, the **Department of Aging** offers **one-on-one Medicare benefit reviews**; call **910-947-4483**. The statewide SHIIP line is **1-855-408-1212**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Call before any Medicare Advantage or Part D decision, especially during open enrollment, **October 15 to December 7, 2026**.

**Help paying Medicare costs and prescriptions.** The **Medicare Savings Programs** can pay Medicare premiums, and **Extra Help** lowers Part D drug costs. SHIIP counselors cover both, and Social Security (**1-800-772-1213**) handles Extra Help. **NC MedAssist (1-866-331-1348)** gives free prescription medicine to **uninsured** North Carolinians with household income **below 300 percent of poverty**. We are not printing income limits for the Medicare programs; ask SHIIP to check them against your parent's numbers.

**Medicaid, and a reason to apply before December 31.** Apply online through ePASS or through Moore DSS at **910-947-2436**. The **NC Medicaid Contact Center** is **888-245-0179**, and the **NC Medicaid Ombudsman** is **1-877-201-3750**. NC Medicaid says that **if you apply by December 31, 2026, Medicaid will cover approved costs for up to 3 months before you applied**. Starting January 1, 2027, that window shrinks to **up to 2 months for adults 65 and older** and people with a disability. If a parent is heading toward a nursing home or already paying for care, that difference can be real money.

**Staying home on Medicaid: CAP/DA.** The **Community Alternatives Program for Disabled Adults (CAP/DA)** is North Carolina's Medicaid waiver that brings in-home aide help, respite and case management so someone who would otherwise qualify for a nursing home can stay home. NC Medicaid's list of case management agencies for Moore County (dated May 2025) names **Divine Home Care, 910-904-2377**; **Gentle Touch Home Care, 910-947-3805**; **Healthkeeperz, 910-522-0009**; **Primary Health Choice, 910-865-3500**; **RBS Case Management, 910-768-0002**; and the **Richmond County Health Department, 910-997-8368**. Moore DSS is not on that list. The list is more than a year old, so if a line does not answer, ask DSS or the Department of Aging who is taking referrals.

**Mental health.** Moore County's public mental health plan is **Trillium Health Resources**. Its **Behavioral Health Crisis Line is 888-302-0738**; member and recipient services is **877-685-2415**.

## PACE

**Two PACE programs list most of Moore County, by ZIP code.**

PACE, the **Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly**, takes over a person's whole care plan, doctors, a day center, therapy, personal care, prescriptions and transportation, so someone who needs a nursing home level of care can keep living at home. NC Medicaid's official PACE list (October 2025) shows:

- **Randolph Health StayWell Senior Care**, **809 Curry Drive, Asheboro**, **336-628-4200**, for **Montgomery, Moore and Randolph** counties. Its Moore ZIP codes on the list are **27242, 27281, 27325, 27376, 28327, 28350, 28374, 28387 and 28394**.
- **LIFE St. Joseph of the Pines**, **4900 Raeford Road, Fayetteville**, **910-429-7242**, for Cumberland and **portions of Moore** and three other counties. Its Moore-area ZIP codes on the list include **28315, 28326 and 28373**.
- **Piedmont Health SeniorCare's Pittsboro center**, **919-545-7341**, also lists **28326**.

The list goes by the ZIP code where the person lives, and some ZIP codes cross county lines. Two Moore ZIP codes, **28370 and 28388**, do not appear on any program's list. **Call the program to confirm it serves your parent's address and has room** before you plan around it.

**If PACE does not fit.** Ask Moore DSS (**910-947-2436**) and the CAP/DA agencies above about **CAP/DA**, and ask the Department of Aging (**910-947-4483**) what else can keep your parent at home.

## Frequently asked questions
**Mom is 70, lives in Southern Pines, and gets about $2,900 a month from Social Security and a small pension. Does she qualify for a tax break?** Likely. About $2,900 a month is about $34,800 a year, under the **$38,800** limit for 2026 and the **$39,900** limit for 2027, as long as that is the whole household's income, including a spouse's. The **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion** takes the greater of **$25,000 or half** her home's value off. File by **June 1**. Call the Tax Department at **910-947-2255**.

**Dad's house gets a new value in 2027. What should we watch for?** The county says the next revaluation is scheduled for **2027**. The chance to appeal ends when the **Board of Equalization and Review** adjourns, so open the value notice right away and call **910-947-2255** if it looks wrong. If he uses the **Circuit Breaker**, he files again every year anyway.

**Should my father use the Circuit Breaker instead?** Go in with open eyes. It caps his tax at 4 or 5 percent of income, but the rest is **deferred, not forgiven**: on a sale, a death or a move, **the current year plus the three most recent years come due with interest**. He needs **five years** of ownership and must **file every year**. If his income is under the exclusion limit, the exclusion is usually simpler. Ask the Tax Department to run both, and talk to the **Senior Legal Helpline (1-877-579-7562)** if the house is part of an estate plan.

**Mom's savings are running out and she may need Medicaid for care. Does timing matter?** It can. NC Medicaid says applications filed **by December 31, 2026** can get approved costs covered for **up to 3 months before** the application; from January 1, 2027 that drops to **2 months** for adults 65 and older. Call Moore DSS at **910-947-2436**, and talk to the Senior Legal Helpline before moving any assets.

**Mom lives alone in Pinehurst and I live out of state. What can I set up today?** Sign up for the **Register of Deeds' recording notifications** and **CodeRED** alerts, and put a **File of Life** on her refrigerator. Call the **Department of Aging (910-947-4483)** about a Medicare review during open enrollment, lunch at the Senior Enrichment Center, medical rides and in-home help, and call **Meals on Wheels of the Sandhills (910-704-9810)** about delivered meals. If she is on Medicaid or needs a nursing home level of care, ask **StayWell Senior Care (336-628-4200)** whether PACE covers her ZIP code.
