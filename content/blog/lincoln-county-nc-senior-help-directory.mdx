## Property tax relief (apply with the Lincoln County Tax Department, 704-736-8550)

**The office.** The **Lincoln County Tax Department**, **100 East Main Street, Lincolnton, NC 28092**, main line **704-736-8542**, open **Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.** The **Tax Administrator is Susan Sain**, and the state's list of county assessors (revised September 23, 2026) shows her as Lincoln's **County Assessor**. Relief applications go to the **Exemptions** desk on the **first floor**, **704-736-8550** (the county also lists **704-736-8549**). The county offers an **online Property Tax Relief Form** linked from each of its relief pages, or you can use the state's paper form.

**The three programs.** All three are set by the state and filed on the state's **Form AV-9** with the county. The county says **open enrollment runs January 1 to June 1** for all three.

- **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion.** It **excludes the greater of $25,000 or 50 percent of the appraised value of the home**. You must be **at least 65, or totally and permanently disabled**, and own the home where you live. The prior year's income for **both the applicant and spouse** must be within the state limit: **$38,800 for the 2026 tax year, $39,900 for 2027**. A disability claim also needs the state's **Certification of Disability (Form AV-9A)**. The county's Seniors/Disabled page mixes two years (it spells out "thirty-seven thousand nine hundred" next to the figure $38,800) and has not been updated for 2027. The state's 2027 AV-9 carries the current figure: **$39,900**.
- **Circuit Breaker Tax Deferment.** Caps the tax at **4 percent of income up to $38,800** and **5 percent from $38,801 to $58,200** for 2026 (for 2027 the ceilings are **$39,900** and **$59,850**). The owner must be **65 or older or totally and permanently disabled**, and the county says the owner must have **owned and lived in the home at least five years**. The county and the state both say **you must apply every year** you want the deferral. **This is a deferral, not forgiveness.** The taxes above the cap are put off, not erased, and a sale, a death or a move can bring them due. Ask the Tax Department exactly what would come due and when. Understand that before your parent signs, especially if the plan is to sell the house or leave it to family.
- **Disabled Veteran Exclusion.** **Excludes the first $45,000** of the appraised value for a veteran with a **100 percent permanent total service-connected disability**, or an unmarried surviving spouse. The county says to bring a **Veteran's Disability Certification** from **Lincoln County Veterans Services, 302 North Academy Street, Lincolnton, 704-736-8506** (below).

**Deadline.** **June 1.** Mark it every year for the Circuit Breaker, and once for the other two.

**The 2027 revaluation is coming.** The county says its revaluations run on a **four-year cycle**, the current values took effect **January 1, 2023**, and **the next revaluation takes effect January 1, 2027**. Every property in the county gets a new value for the 2027 bill. If your parent's new value looks wrong, call the **Appraisal office, 100 East Main Street, third floor, 704-736-8670**, and read the county's **2027 Reappraisal** page for the appeal steps. And if a higher value pushes the bill up, that is the year to check the relief programs above before **June 1, 2027**.

**Rates.** The county's **2026-27** rate is **49.9 cents per $100** of value. The **City of Lincolnton** adds **50 cents**, for a combined **99.9 cents** inside the city. Fire districts add **9.5 to 15 cents**. The county's rate page tells **Maiden** residents to call the Town of Maiden for its rate.

**Bills and payments.** The county says tax notices are **mailed in early July**, and it gives a **2 percent discount on payments made July 1 through August 31**. Taxes are **due September 1** and **delinquent after January 5**; the county says a **2 percent** penalty applies on **January 6**, with interest after that. Pay at the drop box beside the front door of 100 East Main Street, by mail to the same address, online at **lincolncountytax.com**, or by phone at **1-855-683-4525**. A convenience fee applies to card payments. Collections questions: **704-736-8542**.

**Paperwork that protects the house.** The **Lincoln County Register of Deeds**, **Danny R. Hester**, is at **206 Gamble Drive, second floor, Lincolnton**, open Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The office's own site lists **704-736-1744**; the county's staff directory lists **704-736-8530**. A power of attorney that will be used for real estate is recorded there.

## Energy and utility help

**Where to apply: Lincoln County DSS, 704-732-0738.** You can also apply through **ePASS**.

**LIEAP.** A **one-time payment** toward the heating bill. The state and the county both say households with **someone 60 or older**, or a disabled person receiving services through the NC Division of Aging and Adult Services, can sign up from **December 1 to 31**; everyone else from **January 1 to March 31** or until the money runs out. **December 1 is one week away.** Get your parent's income papers and a recent heating bill together now.

**CIP.** The **Crisis Intervention Program** is for a heating or cooling emergency, taken **July 1 to June 30** or until the money runs out. The state says a household with a member **60 or older** or disabled can have **resources at or below $4,500**. Apply at DSS or through ePASS.

**Which electric company.** The county's own outage notices point Lincoln residents to **Duke Energy**, **Rutherford EMC** and **EnergyUnited**, and the **City of Lincolnton** runs its own utilities (billing at **114 West Sycamore Street, 704-736-8980**, Monday to Friday 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.). Check your parent's bill to see which one it is.
- **EnergyUnited members.** The EnergyUnited Foundation and its Operation Round-Up fund help with **urgent needs for housing, utilities and humane needs**. Apply at **EnergyUnited.com/euf-application** or call **1-800-522-3793**.
- **Rutherford EMC members.** The co-op's list of helping agencies for Lincoln County names **Lincoln County DSS (704-732-0738)**, **Christian Ministries (704-732-0383)**, **East Lincoln Christian Ministry (704-483-4415)** and **Lincoln County Community Action (704-748-9744)**.
- **Duke Energy and City of Lincolnton customers.** Call the company and ask about payment arrangements, and ask DSS about CIP.

**Too hot, not just too cold.** The state's **Operation Fan Heat Relief** list names **Lincoln County DSS, 704-736-8588**, as Lincoln's contact. The season has ended; note it for next summer.

## Medicare, Medicaid, prescriptions

**Free Medicare counseling, in Lincolnton.** North Carolina's **Seniors' Health Insurance Information Program (SHIIP)**, run by the NC Department of Insurance, gives free, unbiased Medicare counseling at **1-855-408-1212**. In Lincoln County, Senior Services has trained SHIIP counselors: call **Gretchen McClure at 704-736-8811** for an appointment. **Medicare open enrollment ends December 7.** Call this week, and bring the **Annual Notice of Change** that arrived in the fall. The senior center's November calendar also lists a **Medicare Fraud and Abuse** talk on **Monday, November 30, at 11 a.m.**

**Help paying Medicare costs and prescriptions.** The **Medicare Savings Programs** can pay Medicare premiums, and **Extra Help** lowers Part D drug costs; Social Security (**1-800-772-1213**) handles Extra Help and SHIIP can screen for both. We are not printing income limits for the Medicare or Medicaid programs; ask SHIIP or DSS to check them against your parent's numbers.

**Medicaid, and a reason to apply before December 31.** Apply online through ePASS or at DSS; the county's Medicaid line is **704-736-8590**. The **NC Medicaid Contact Center** is **888-245-0179**, and the **NC Medicaid Ombudsman** is **1-877-201-3750**. NC Medicaid says that **if you apply by December 31, 2026, Medicaid will cover approved costs for up to 3 months before you applied**. Starting January 1, 2027, that window shrinks to **up to 2 months for adults 65 and older** and people with a disability. If a parent is heading toward a nursing home or already paying for care, that difference can be real money. Talk to the Senior Legal Helpline (below) before anyone moves money or the house.

**Staying home on Medicaid: CAP/DA.** The **Community Alternatives Program for Disabled Adults (CAP/DA)** is North Carolina's Medicaid waiver that brings case management and in-home help to someone who would otherwise qualify for a nursing home. NC Medicaid's list of case management agencies for Lincoln County names **Atrium Health Cleveland-Care Solutions, 980-487-4777**; **Healthkeeperz, 910-522-0009**; **KBHR Consulting Services, 704-962-1267**; **Lincoln County DSS, 704-732-0738**; **Personal Home Care of North Carolina, 704-522-6149**; **Primary Health Choice, 910-865-3500**; **RHA Management Services, 888-207-7828**; and **Walkers Home Care CAPDA, 252-432-6308**. Ask each about openings and waits.

**Mental health.** Lincoln County's public mental health plan is **Vaya Partners**. Its **Behavioral Health Crisis Line is 833-353-2093**; member and recipient services is **888-235-4673**.

## Frequently asked questions
**Mom is 74, lives in Denver, and gets about $2,900 a month from Social Security and a small pension. Does she qualify for a tax break?** Likely. About $2,900 a month is about $34,800 a year, under the **$38,800** limit for 2026 and the **$39,900** limit for 2027, as long as that is the whole household's income, including a spouse's. The **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion** takes the greater of **$25,000 or half** her home's value off. With the **2027 revaluation** coming, it matters more next year. Apply between **January 1 and June 1**. Call the exemptions desk at **704-736-8550**.

**Should my father use the Circuit Breaker instead?** Go in with open eyes. It caps his tax at 4 or 5 percent of income, but the rest is **deferred, not forgiven**, and a sale, a death or a move can bring it due. He must **apply every year**, and the county says he needs **five years** of owning and living in the home. If his income is under the exclusion limit, the exclusion is usually simpler. Ask the Tax Department to run both, and talk to the **Senior Legal Helpline (1-877-579-7562)** if the house is part of an estate plan.

**Dad's house is getting a new value in 2027. What should he do?** Watch the mail for the new value early in 2027, read the county's **2027 Reappraisal** page, and call the Appraisal office at **704-736-8670** if the number looks wrong. Then check the relief programs before **June 1, 2027**.

**Mom's savings are running out and she may need Medicaid for care. Does timing matter?** It can. NC Medicaid says applications filed **by December 31, 2026** can get approved costs covered for **up to 3 months before** the application; from January 1, 2027 that drops to **2 months** for adults 65 and older. Call Lincoln DSS at **704-736-8590**, and talk to the Senior Legal Helpline before moving any assets.

**Dad has dementia and Mom is wearing out. Where does she get a break?** Call the caregiver program at **704-736-8410** about respite and the monthly support group. Call **Project C.A.R.E. at 704-432-1111** for dementia-specific help. If they live in a PACE ZIP code, ask PACE about day center care.

**Mom lives alone in Vale and I live out of state. What can I set up today?** Call Senior Services (**704-732-9053**) about meal vouchers and a SHIIP Medicare review before December 7 (**704-736-8811**), and ask about senior rides (**704-736-8492**).
