Hours: **Monday 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.; Tuesday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.** Main line **704-216-7714**. The Centralina Area Agency on Aging names Rufty-Holmes as its local contact for Rowan.

## Property tax relief (apply with the Rowan County Tax Assessor, 704-216-8565)

**The office.** Senior and disability relief is handled by the **Rowan County Tax Assessor's Office**, **402 North Main Street, Suite 201, Salisbury, NC 28144**, **Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.** The main line is **704-216-8558**, and the county lists **704-216-8565** for **Exemptions**, the desk that handles relief. The state's official list of county assessors (revised September 23, 2026) names **Wendell Main** as Rowan's assessor at the same North Main Street address and **704-216-8558**. The county's application is posted on its Exemptions page as the "Application for Elderly, Disabled, and or Veteran Property Tax Relief."

**The three programs.** All three are set by the state and filed on the state's **Form AV-9** with the county.

- **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion.** The county says it **excludes the greater of the first $25,000 or 50 percent of the appraised value** of the permanent residence. You must be **at least 65 or totally and permanently disabled**, and household income must be within the limit: **$38,800 for the 2026 tax year, $39,900 for 2027**. The county's page does not say whether you reapply each year, so ask the Exemptions desk, and tell the Assessor if your parent moves, sells or rents the house.
- **Circuit Breaker Property Tax Deferment.** Limits the tax to **4 percent of income at $38,800 or less** and **5 percent above $38,800 up to $58,200** for 2026 (the 2027 ceiling is **$59,850**). The owner must have **owned and occupied the property at least five years**, be 65 or older or totally and permanently disabled, and **every co-owner must qualify**. **This is a deferral, not forgiveness.** The county says that after a **disqualifying event**, such as a transfer, a death, or the home no longer being the permanent residence, **the most recent three years of deferred taxes come due with interest**. And the county says **you must apply each and every year** you want to defer. Understand that before your parent signs, especially if the plan is to sell the house or leave it to family.
- **Disabled Veteran Exclusion.** **Excludes up to the first $45,000** of the appraised value for an honorably discharged veteran with a **100 percent total and permanent service-connected disability**, or an unmarried surviving spouse. The county says **there is no age or income limitation**. Rowan County Veterans Services (below) can help with the paperwork.

**Deadline.** The county says **applications must be filed by June 1**.

**The 2027 revaluation is coming.** Rowan County revalues **every four years**. Today's values took effect **January 1, 2023**, and the county says **the next revaluation will take place on January 1, 2027**, with the one after that planned for 2031. The county says informal appeals **typically take place between January and March**, and the **Board of Equalization and Review** first meets between the first Monday in April and the first Monday in May; appeals must be in before that board adjourns. Parts of the county's revaluation page still describe the 2023 appeal forms and deadlines, and it has **not posted a 2027 appeal deadline**, so call the revaluation line, **704-216-8586**, when the new value arrives.

The county's pages do not say how a revaluation affects relief that is already approved, so call **704-216-8565** once the new value arrives to confirm your parent's status. If a parent's income is under the 2027 limit and they are not yet on the exclusion, **June 1, 2027** is the date to circle.

**Rates and bills.** The county rate on **2026 bills is 58 cents per $100** of value. Towns add their own: **Salisbury 68.4 cents**, **Kannapolis 55.95**, **China Grove 56**, **Landis 49**, **Spencer 63.05**, **East Spencer 86**, **Rockwell 55**, **Granite Quarry 49**, **Faith 50**, and **Cleveland 35** (Cleveland also carries a 13 cent fire district). Downtown Salisbury adds a 15.6 cent district. Bills are mailed in **July**. The county's Tax Collector says taxes are **due September 1 and payable through January 5**, with a **1.5 percent discount in July** and **1 percent in August**; interest starts at **2 percent in January** and adds **three quarters of a percent each month** after. The Tax Collector is at **402 North Main Street, Suite 101**, **704-216-8544**.

**One protection worth ten minutes.** The **Rowan County Register of Deeds** offers **Property Check**, a **free 24/7 fraud alert** that tells you by your chosen contact method **any time a filing occurs** on a property or owner you name. The county is clear that it **does not stop** fraudulent activity; it tells you fast so you can act. The office is at **402 North Main Street, Salisbury**, **704-216-8626**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Veterans who register their DD-214 there also get a free **Thank A Vet** ID card for local discounts.

## Energy and utility help

**Where to apply: Rowan County DSS.** North Carolina's two heating and cooling programs are applied for through **ePASS** or the county DSS, **1813 East Innes Street, Salisbury**, **704-216-8330**. Rowan County does not post its own energy assistance page, so call to ask how intake works this season.

**LIEAP.** A **once-a-year payment** toward heating bills, sent straight to the vendor. The state says households with **someone 60 or older**, or a disabled person getting services through the NC Division of Aging and Adult Services, can sign up **December 1 to 31**; everyone else applies **January 1 to March 31** or until the money runs out. Income is based on **130 percent** of the poverty level.

**CIP.** The **Crisis Intervention Program** is for heating or cooling emergencies, taken **July 1 to June 30** or until the county's money runs out. The state sets income at **150 percent** of poverty, and households with a member **60 or older** or disabled can have **resources at or below $4,500**. The state says a disconnect notice is **not required at this time**; a final notice or past-due bill is accepted, and **no face-to-face interview is required**. You can apply by mail, email, fax, drop-off at DSS, or ePASS. Rowan County does not post a dollar cap, so ask DSS.

**Rowan Helping Ministries.** Its Crisis Assistance Network, **704-637-6838**, option 1, is the county's main nonprofit door for crisis help. Call to ask what it can cover this month.

**If the bill says City of Salisbury (water and sewer).** The city's **Share2Care** program helps people **whose finances limit their ability to keep water or sewer service**. The city says **applications go directly to Rowan Helping Ministries**. The city also offers **payment plans for past-due balances**, and points to the state's water assistance through Rowan DSS at **704-216-8330**.

**If the bill says Town of Landis.** Landis runs its own public power. The town points residents who need help to **Rowan County DSS (704-216-8330)** and **Rowan Helping Ministries (704-637-6838)**. Town Hall is **704-857-2411**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

**Too hot, not just too cold.** The state's **Operation Fan Heat Relief** gives fans, and air conditioners for some qualifying medical conditions, to people **60 and older** and adults with disabilities, but only **through October 31, 2026**. Rowan County's provider is **Rufty-Holmes Senior Center, 704-216-7714**.

## Medicare, Medicaid, prescriptions

**Free Medicare counseling.** North Carolina's **Seniors' Health Insurance Information Program (SHIIP)**, run by the NC Department of Insurance, gives free, unbiased Medicare counseling. The state's county locator lists **Rufty-Holmes Senior Center, 1120 South Martin Luther King Jr. Avenue, Salisbury, 704-216-7704**, **by appointment only** (ask for SHIIP help). The statewide SHIIP line is **1-855-408-1212**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Call before any Medicare Advantage or Part D decision, especially during open enrollment, **October 15 to December 7, 2026**.

**Help paying Medicare costs and prescriptions.** The **Medicare Savings Programs** can pay Medicare premiums, and **Extra Help** lowers Part D drug costs. Ask Rowan DSS's Economic Services at **704-216-8330** about the Medicare Savings Programs, and SHIIP or Social Security (**1-800-772-1213**) about Extra Help. **NC MedAssist (1-866-331-1348)** gives free prescription medicine to **uninsured** North Carolinians with household income **below 300 percent of poverty**. We are not printing income limits for the Medicare programs; ask SHIIP to check them against your parent's numbers.

**Medicaid, and a reason to apply before December 31.** Apply through Rowan DSS at **704-216-8330**, online through ePASS, or by fax to **704-216-7937**. The **NC Medicaid Contact Center** is **888-245-0179**. NC Medicaid says that **if you apply by December 31, 2026, Medicaid will cover approved costs for up to 3 months before you applied**. Starting January 1, 2027, that window shrinks to **up to 2 months for adults 65 and older** and people with a disability. If a parent is heading toward a nursing home or already paying for care, that difference can be real money.

**Staying home on Medicaid: CAP/DA.** The **Community Alternatives Program for Disabled Adults (CAP/DA)** is North Carolina's Medicaid waiver that brings in-home aide help, respite and case management so someone who would otherwise qualify for a nursing home can stay home. NC Medicaid's list of case management agencies for Rowan County (dated May 2025) names **Primary Health Choice, 910-865-3500**; **RBS Case Management, 910-768-0002**; **RHA Management Services, 888-207-7828**; **Rowan Regional Medical Center, Novant Health, 704-210-5620**; and **Walkers Home Care CAPDA, 252-432-6308**. The list is more than a year old, so if a line does not answer, start with DSS.

**In-home help through DSS.** Rowan DSS's **In-Home Aide** program gives **personal care and home management** help to older adults and adults with disabilities; the county notes **a wait list**. Call **704-216-8331**.

**Special Assistance In-Home.** An alternative to moving into an adult care home for **Medicaid-eligible** adults with income **at or below 100 percent of poverty**. It needs an assessment by an Adult Services social worker and an **FL-2** from a physician. The county says **slots are limited and there is a waiting list**. Call **704-216-8330**.

**Mental health.** Rowan County's public mental health plan is **Vaya Health** (not the plan most neighboring counties use). Its **Behavioral Health Crisis Line is 1-800-849-6127, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week**; the member and recipient service line is **1-800-962-9003**, Monday to Saturday 7 a.m. to 6 p.m.

## PACE

**Most of Rowan County is in a PACE service area, but the center is in Lexington.**

PACE, the **Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly**, takes over a person's whole care plan, doctors, a day center, therapy, personal care, prescriptions and transportation, so someone who needs a nursing home level of care can keep living at home. NC Medicaid's official PACE list (October 2025) names **Carolina SeniorCare**, **802 East Center Street, Lexington, NC 27292**, **336-746-3500**, for **portions of Rowan County**, with coverage set by the ZIP code where your parent lives.

**Who qualifies.** Medicare says PACE is for people **55 and older** who **live in a PACE service area**, **need a nursing home level of care as certified by the state**, and **can live safely in the community** with PACE's help. Medicare says that **if you have Medicaid, you won't pay a monthly premium for PACE**.

**Check the ZIP code.** The state's list includes these Rowan ZIP codes: **Salisbury 28144, 28146 and 28147**, **China Grove 28023**, **Gold Hill 28071**, **Kannapolis 28081 and 28083**, **Landis 28088**, **Mount Ulla 28125**, **Richfield 28137**, **Rockwell 28138** and **Spencer 28159**. It does **not** list **Granite Quarry 28072**, **East Spencer 28039**, **Faith 28041** or the Salisbury post office box ZIP **28145**. Some of these ZIP codes cross into Cabarrus and Stanly counties, where a different PACE program also appears on the state's list, so confirm your parent's address with Carolina SeniorCare when you call.

## Frequently asked questions
**Mom is 72, lives in China Grove, and gets about $2,900 a month from Social Security and a small pension. Does she qualify for a tax break?** Likely. About $2,900 a month is about $34,800 a year, under the **$38,800** limit for 2026 and the **$39,900** limit for 2027, as long as that is the whole household's income. The **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion** takes the greater of **$25,000 or half** her home's appraised value off. The application is due **by June 1**. Call the Exemptions desk at **704-216-8565**.

**Dad's house gets a new value in 2027. What should we watch for?** The county says the revaluation takes place **January 1, 2027**, and informal appeals typically run **January to March**. If the value looks wrong, call **704-216-8586** right away. If he uses the **Circuit Breaker**, he files again every year anyway.

**Should my father use the Circuit Breaker instead?** Go in with open eyes. It limits his tax to 4 or 5 percent of income, but the rest is **deferred, not forgiven**: after his death, a transfer, or a move, **the most recent three years come due with interest**. He needs **5 years** of ownership and occupancy, every co-owner must qualify, and he must **reapply every year**. If his income is under the exclusion limit, the exclusion is usually simpler. Ask the Assessor's office to run both, and talk to the **Senior Legal Helpline (1-877-579-7562)** if the house is part of an estate plan.

**Mom's savings are running out and she may need Medicaid for care. Does timing matter?** It can. NC Medicaid says applications filed **by December 31, 2026** can get approved costs covered for **up to 3 months before** the application; from January 1, 2027 that drops to **2 months** for adults 65 and older. Call Rowan DSS at **704-216-8330**, and talk to the Senior Legal Helpline before moving any assets.

**Mom lives alone in Salisbury and I live out of state. What can I set up today?** Sign her up for **Property Check** at the Register of Deeds and for **RoCo Alerts**. Call **Meals on Wheels of Rowan (704-633-0352)**, or register her for a free **Lunch Club** at **704-216-7702**. In Salisbury, the bus is **50 cents at 60** with a photo ID. Ask **Rufty-Holmes (704-216-7704)** about Medicare counseling and caregiver support. And if she needs a nursing home level of care, ask **Carolina SeniorCare (336-746-3500)** whether her ZIP code is covered by PACE.
