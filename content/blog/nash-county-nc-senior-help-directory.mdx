## Property tax relief (apply with the Nash County Tax Department, 252-459-1222)

**The office.** The **Nash County Tax Department**, **120 W. Washington Street, Suite 2058, Nashville, NC 27856**, main line **252-459-9824**, open **Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.** The county says its assessor and collector offices are combined into one office that lists, appraises and collects, and that it "processes applications for tax relief." For an application, the county says to call **252-459-1222** or use its Tax Forms page. The state's official list of county assessors (revised September 23, 2026) shows **Shameeka Tillery** as Nash's **Interim County Assessor** at the same Suite 2058 address, **252-459-1218**.

**The three programs.** All three are set by the state and filed on the state's **Form AV-9** with the county. The county also asks for a **certification of disability** form for a disability claim and a **disabled veteran certification** form for the veteran exclusion; both are on the county's Tax Exemptions page.

- **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion.** It **excludes the greater of $25,000 or 50 percent of the appraised value of the home**. You must be **at least 65, or totally and permanently disabled**, and own the home where you live. The prior year's income for **both the applicant and spouse** must be within the state limit: **$38,800 for the 2026 tax year, $39,900 for 2027**. The county's page still shows the **2025** limit of **$37,900**; the state's AV-9 forms carry the current figures. The county says unmarried joint owners each apply separately, and that you **do not have to apply every year** once you are approved.
- **Circuit Breaker Tax Deferment.** Caps the tax at **4 percent of income up to $38,800** and **5 percent from $38,801 to $58,200** for 2026 (for 2027 the ceilings are **$39,900** and **$59,850**). The owner must be **65 or older or totally and permanently disabled**, and the state's form says in capital letters that **you must file a new application every year**. **This is a deferral, not forgiveness.** The county says the taxes over the cap **remain a lien on the property**, and **the last three years of deferred taxes become due, with interest,** when the owner dies, transfers the property or stops living there. Understand that before your parent signs, especially if the plan is to sell the house or leave it to family.
- **Disabled Veteran Exclusion.** **Excludes up to the first $45,000** of the assessed value for an honorably discharged veteran who is **100 percent totally and permanently disabled** from a service-connected cause, or who received VA benefits for specially adapted housing, or an unmarried surviving spouse. The county says there is **no age or income limitation**. **Nash County Veterans Services** (below) can help with the certification.

**Deadline.** **June 1.** Mark it every year for the Circuit Breaker, and once for the other two.

**No new reappraisal this year.** Nash County's last countywide revaluation took effect in **2024**. The county's **2026-27 budget ordinance**, adopted in June 2026, says the revenue-neutral rate "was published after the 2024 revaluation year" and sets the county rate at **63 cents per $100**, the same rate the county's 2025 rate sheet shows. The state laws passed in 2026 about delaying 2026 reappraisals do not touch Nash's bill this year. The county's Property Revaluations page still describes the last revaluation in the present tense; if you want to question your parent's value, call **252-459-9824** and ask what can be reviewed for 2027.

**Town rates.** Towns and fire districts add their own rates on top of the county's. The county's **2025** rate sheet shows, for example, **Nashville 59 cents**, **City of Rocky Mount 62 cents**, **Spring Hope 67 cents**, **Bailey 80 cents** and fire districts from **7 to 15 cents**, plus a **$155** solid waste fee. Ask the Tax Department for the current year's numbers.

**Bills and payments.** The county says tax bills are **mailed in August** and taxes are **due September 1**. Under state law (G.S. 105-360), taxes can be paid at face value **until January 6**; interest starts at **2 percent on January 6**. The county also accepts **prepayments** toward a future bill; call the Tax Department about how. Pay online through the county's payment portal, or by phone through Official Payments at **1-800-272-9829** (jurisdiction code **4314**); a convenience fee applies to both.

**Paperwork that protects the house.** The **Nash County Register of Deeds**, **Sandra Davis**, is at **120 W. Washington Street, Suite 3030, Nashville**, **252-459-9836**, open Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. A power of attorney that will be used for real estate is recorded there.

## Energy and utility help

**Where to apply: Nash County DSS.** The county names **Kimberly Nicholson, 252-459-1286**, as the manager for energy programs. You can also apply through **ePASS**.

**LIEAP.** A **one-time payment** toward the heating bill. The state says households with **someone 60 or older**, or a disabled person receiving services through the NC Division of Aging and Adult Services, can sign up from **December 1 to 31**; everyone else from **January 1 to March 31** or until the money runs out. December 1 is two weeks away. Get your parent's income papers and a recent heating bill together now.

**CIP.** The **Crisis Intervention Program** is for a heating or cooling emergency, taken **July 1 to June 30**. The state says a household with a member **60 or older** or disabled can have **resources at or below $4,500**. Apply at DSS or through ePASS.

**Duke Energy Progress customers.** **Share the Light** helps pay energy bills, deposits and reconnection charges. The company says to apply through your county DSS, ePASS, or the **NCDHHS Customer Service Center, 1-800-662-7030, then press 3**.

**City of Rocky Mount utility customers (a city program).** Rocky Mount Public Utilities serves customers in both Nash and Edgecombe counties; billing is **252-972-1250**. The city's **WARM** program gives a **one-time payment of up to $200** toward a city electric or gas heating bill for customers who are **60 or older**, disabled or recently laid off, with income at or below **150 percent of poverty** and a documented crisis. The city says it runs **January through May** and requires an energy audit and a short education video. Call **252-972-1250**.

**The electric co-ops.** **Halifax EMC** (**208 West Whitfield Street, Enfield, 252-445-5111**) serves parts of Nash County, and its **Operation Round Up** page says **individual members can apply for grants**. **Wake Electric** (**919-863-6300**) lists Nash among its counties; its Operation Roundup money goes to the **WECare Foundation**, which lists financial help for members facing hardship. ## Medicare, Medicaid, prescriptions

**Free Medicare counseling, in Nashville and Bailey.** North Carolina's **Seniors' Health Insurance Information Program (SHIIP)**, run by the NC Department of Insurance, gives free, unbiased Medicare counseling at **1-855-408-1212**, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. In Nash County, trained SHIIP counselors work out of the senior centers: call **252-459-7681** for the Nashville center or **252-462-2034** for the Southern Nash center in Bailey. **Medicare open enrollment ends December 7.** Call this week, and bring the **Annual Notice of Change** that arrived in the fall.

**Help paying Medicare costs and prescriptions.** The **Medicare Savings Programs** can pay Medicare premiums, and **Extra Help** lowers Part D drug costs; Social Security (**1-800-772-1213**) handles Extra Help and SHIIP can screen for both. **NC MedAssist (1-866-331-1348)** gives free prescription medicine to uninsured North Carolinians with low income. We are not printing income limits for the Medicare or Medicaid programs; ask SHIIP or DSS to check them against your parent's numbers.

**Medicaid, and a reason to apply before December 31.** Apply online through ePASS or at DSS; the county's Medicaid line is **252-459-1464**. The **NC Medicaid Contact Center** is **888-245-0179**, and the **NC Medicaid Ombudsman** is **1-877-201-3750**. NC Medicaid says that **if you apply by December 31, 2026, Medicaid will cover approved costs for up to 3 months before you applied**. Starting January 1, 2027, that window shrinks to **up to 2 months for adults 65 and older** and people with a disability. If a parent is heading toward a nursing home or already paying for care, that difference can be real money. Talk to the Senior Legal Helpline (below) before anyone moves money or the house.

**Staying home on Medicaid: CAP/DA.** The **Community Alternatives Program for Disabled Adults (CAP/DA)** is North Carolina's Medicaid waiver that brings case management and in-home help to someone who would otherwise qualify for a nursing home. NC Medicaid's list of case management agencies for Nash County names **Amazing Adults of NC, 919-435-6970**; **Healthkeeperz, 910-522-0009**; the **Nash County Health Department, 252-462-2743**; **Primary Health Choice, 910-865-3500**; **RHA Management Services, 888-207-7828**; **Spectrum Support Services, 919-954-1171**; and **Walkers Home Care CAPDA, 252-432-6308**. Ask each about openings and waits.

**Mental health.** Nash County's public mental health plan is **Trillium Health Resources**. Its **Behavioral Health Crisis Line is 888-302-0738**; member and recipient services is **877-685-2415**.

## PACE

**No PACE program serves Nash County.**

PACE, the **Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly**, takes over a person's whole care plan so someone who needs a nursing home level of care can keep living at home. NC Medicaid's official list of the state's **11 PACE organizations** does not include **Nash County** or any Nash ZIP code. No program on the list serves **Edgecombe, Wilson or Halifax** either, so there is no nearby service area to move into on the Rocky Mount side.

**What to use instead.** Ask a CAP/DA case management agency (the list is under Medicare and Medicaid above, including the **Nash County Health Department, 252-462-2743**) about in-home Medicaid help. Ask Senior Services (**252-459-1367**) about home-delivered or frozen meals, and the caregiver program (**252-459-1691**) about respite. Look at **The Wright Center** adult day program in Rocky Mount (**252-442-8363**). Those are the tools that keep a parent at home in Nash County.

## Frequently asked questions
**Mom is 74, lives in Spring Hope, and gets about $2,900 a month from Social Security and a small pension. Does she qualify for a tax break?** Likely. About $2,900 a month is about $34,800 a year, under the **$38,800** limit for 2026 and the **$39,900** limit for 2027, as long as that is the whole household's income, including a spouse's. The **Elderly or Disabled Exclusion** takes the greater of **$25,000 or half** her home's value off. File Form AV-9 by **June 1**. Call the Tax Department at **252-459-1222** for the application.

**Should my father use the Circuit Breaker instead?** Go in with open eyes. It caps his tax at 4 or 5 percent of income, but the rest is **deferred, not forgiven**: on a sale, a death or a move, **the last three years of deferred taxes come due with interest**, and he must **file every year**. If his income is under the exclusion limit, the exclusion is usually simpler. Ask the Tax Department to run both, and talk to the **Senior Legal Helpline (1-877-579-7562)** if the house is part of an estate plan.

**Dad lives in Rocky Mount. Which county helps him?** It depends on the street. Rocky Mount is split between Nash and Edgecombe counties. His property tax bill names the county.
