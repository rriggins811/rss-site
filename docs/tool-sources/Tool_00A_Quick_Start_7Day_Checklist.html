Quick Start: Your First 7 Days

Senior Transition Blueprint | Module 0

Don't try to do everything at once. This checklist maps out your first week in small, manageable steps. Each task takes 20-30 minutes. Check them off as you go.

Days 1-2: Figure Out Where You Are

  • Complete Module 1: identify your transition stage and timeline
  • Fill out the Starting Point Quick Assessment (Module 1 toolkit)
  • Fill out the Timeline Reality Check (Module 1 toolkit)
  • Decide your Blueprint path: Linear, Urgent, or Early Planning

Days 3-4: Address Safety First

  • Walk through the home: identify loose rugs, poor lighting, cluttered walkways
  • Fix anything that could cause a fall TODAY (30 minutes, no module needed)
  • If the senior lives alone: download SeniorSafe at app.seniorsafeapp.com
  • Set up the daily I'm Okay check-in so family knows the senior is safe

Days 5-6: Get the Family on the Same Page

  • Send the Family Sharing Letter to siblings and involved family members
  • Read the Module 13 overview on family communication (prevents blowups later)
  • Start a shared group text or note with family to keep everyone informed
  • Upload completed tools to the SeniorSafe vault so family can access them

Day 7: Build Your Rhythm

  • Schedule recurring Blueprint time (even 30 min twice a week adds up fast)
  • Begin Module 2 (Decluttering) if on the Linear or Early Planning path
  • Begin Module 5 (Safety & Repairs) if on the Urgent path
  • Celebrate: you've made more progress in 7 days than most families make in 6 months

YOUR BLUEPRINT PATH

Linear (most families): Modules in order, 2-3 per month. Comprehensive preparation.

Urgent (crisis mode): Skip to Modules 5, 9, 10. Safety and the home sale first. Come back to the earlier modules later.

Early Planning (1-5+ years): Modules 1-4 at a relaxed pace. Gradual decluttering and financial prep.
